# Glossary of terms, version 0.1 (draft)

This is the controlled word list of the standard. A name may only use short forms that are listed here, or tokens added under the extension rules in `standard.md`.

There are 230 terms: 215 in the core and 15 in the South African extension set.

## How to read an entry

- **Short form** is the heading. It is the token that appears in names. One short form means one concept, and one concept has one short form.
- **Full term** is the plain English term to use in descriptions.
- **Slot** says where the token may sit in a name. The slots are explained in `standard.md`.
- **Also called** lists other words people use for the same concept. They all map to this one short form. Search for them here to find the token.
- **Definition** says exactly what the term means in this standard.
- **Not to be confused with** names the terms it is most often mixed up with.
- **Source checked** names the public text the definition was checked against, where there is one. Definitions are written in this standard's own words and do not quote the source.
- **Related terms** links to the other entries in the same family of ideas. Every link goes both ways.

## Time points and time bases

Tokens for the TIME slot. They say when a value is measured or over what span a rate applies.

### BOP

- **Full term:** Start of period
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Also called:** beginning of period, opening
- **Definition:** The instant at the start of the model period, before anything happens in that period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not T0, which is one fixed date. BOP moves with each projection period.
- **Related terms:** [EOP](#eop) end of period, [MOP](#mop) middle of period, [T0](#t0) valuation date, [INC](#inc) inception, [BEF](#bef) before, [AFT](#aft) after

### EOP

- **Full term:** End of period
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Also called:** closing
- **Definition:** The instant at the end of the model period, after everything in that period has happened unless a BEF clause says otherwise.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not EXPY (expiry of a policy) and not MAT (maturity). EOP is about the model period, not the policy.
- **Related terms:** [BOP](#bop) start of period, [MOP](#mop) middle of period, [T0](#t0) valuation date, [BEF](#bef) before, [AFT](#aft) after

### MOP

- **Full term:** Middle of period
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Also called:** mid-period
- **Definition:** The instant half way through the model period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not AVG. A mid-period value is measured at one instant. An average is taken over the whole period.
- **Related terms:** [BOP](#bop) start of period, [EOP](#eop) end of period, [AVG](#avg) average

### T0

- **Full term:** Valuation date
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Also called:** time zero, calculation date, reporting date
- **Definition:** The fixed date at which the projection starts. Values at T0 do not vary by projection period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INC, which is the start of each policy. Not BOP, which moves with the period.
- **Related terms:** [BOP](#bop) start of period, [EOP](#eop) end of period, [INC](#inc) inception, [TIME](#time) projection time, [DATE](#date) calendar date

### INC

- **Full term:** Inception
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Also called:** issue, entry, commencement, outset
- **Definition:** The date on which the policy starts. A value at INC is fixed for the life of the policy.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INCM (income benefit). Not INIT, which describes costs that arise around inception. Not T0.
- **Related terms:** [T0](#t0) valuation date, [BOP](#bop) start of period, [INIT](#init) initial, [NB](#nb) new business, [DUR](#dur) policy duration, [WAITP](#waitp) waiting period

### BEF

- **Full term:** Before
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Definition:** Marks a value measured before the named item is taken into account. It is always followed by exactly one token.
- **Not to be confused with:** It does not mean an earlier period. It orders events inside one time point or one calculation.
- **Related terms:** [AFT](#aft) after, [BOP](#bop) start of period, [EOP](#eop) end of period

### AFT

- **Full term:** After
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Definition:** Marks a value measured after the named item is taken into account. It is always followed by exactly one token.
- **Not to be confused with:** It does not mean a later period. It orders events inside one time point or one calculation.
- **Related terms:** [BEF](#bef) before, [BOP](#bop) start of period, [EOP](#eop) end of period

### ANN

- **Full term:** Annual
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Also called:** per year, yearly, annualised
- **Definition:** Time base of one year. On a rate it means the rate applies over a year. On an amount it means the amount is expressed per year.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ANTY (annuity). Not PRD: an annual value in a monthly model is not the value for the model period.
- **Related terms:** [MTH](#mth) monthly, [PRD](#prd) per model period, [RATE](#rate) rate

### MTH

- **Full term:** Monthly
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Also called:** per month
- **Definition:** Time base of one calendar month.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not MAT (maturity). Not PRD unless the model period happens to be a month.
- **Related terms:** [ANN](#ann) annual, [PRD](#prd) per model period, [RATE](#rate) rate

### PRD

- **Full term:** Per model period
- **Slot:** TIME
- **Also called:** per time step
- **Definition:** Time base equal to one model period, whatever its length. Use it only when the period is not fixed at a month or a year.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PER, which builds a ratio. Not PERS (persistency).
- **Related terms:** [ANN](#ann) annual, [MTH](#mth) monthly, [RATE](#rate) rate

## Time and age items

Items that measure time itself.

### TIME

- **Full term:** Projection time
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** t, projection period number
- **Definition:** The number of model periods elapsed since the valuation date.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DUR, which counts from policy inception. Not TERM, which is a contractual length.
- **Related terms:** [DUR](#dur) policy duration, [TERM](#term) policy term, [T0](#t0) valuation date, [DATE](#date) calendar date, [AGE](#age) age

### DUR

- **Full term:** Policy duration
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** duration in force, policy year, curtate duration
- **Definition:** The time elapsed since the policy started, measured at the stated time point.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not TERM (the full contractual length). Not the financial sense of duration as interest rate sensitivity, which this version does not cover.
- **Related terms:** [TIME](#time) projection time, [TERM](#term) policy term, [INC](#inc) inception, [REM](#rem) remaining, [AGE](#age) age, [DPP](#dpp) discounted payback period

### TERM

- **Full term:** Policy term
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** contract term, benefit term
- **Definition:** The contractual length of the policy or of a named feature, from start to scheduled end.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DUR (time elapsed so far). Not TMNL (terminal bonus). Not termination, for which see EXIT.
- **Related terms:** [DUR](#dur) policy duration, [REM](#rem) remaining, [MAT](#mat) maturity, [EXPY](#expy) expiry, [TIME](#time) projection time, [DEFP](#defp) deferred period, [WAITP](#waitp) waiting period

### REM

- **Full term:** Remaining
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** outstanding, unexpired
- **Definition:** Narrows a length of time or an amount to the part that is still to come after the stated time point.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FUT, which sums future values of a flow. REM narrows a single contractual quantity such as a term.
- **Related terms:** [TERM](#term) policy term, [DUR](#dur) policy duration, [FUT](#fut) future total

### AGE

- **Full term:** Age
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** attained age, age at entry
- **Definition:** The age of the life assured at the stated time point. The description must say which age definition the model uses.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DUR. Age at entry is AGE at INC. Attained age is AGE at BOP or EOP.
- **Related terms:** [DUR](#dur) policy duration, [TIME](#time) projection time, [LIFE](#life) life assured, [DATE](#date) calendar date

### DATE

- **Full term:** Calendar date
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** A calendar date for the stated time point.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not TIME, which is a count of periods and not a date.
- **Related terms:** [TIME](#time) projection time, [T0](#t0) valuation date, [AGE](#age) age

### DEFP

- **Full term:** Deferred period
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** elimination period
- **Definition:** The length of time a life must be continuously disabled before an income benefit starts to be paid.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not WAITP, which runs from the start of the policy. Not a deferred annuity's deferment, which this version does not name.
- **Related terms:** [WAITP](#waitp) waiting period, [INCM](#incm) income benefit, [DIS](#dis) disability, [TERM](#term) policy term

### WAITP

- **Full term:** Waiting period
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** no-claim period, exclusion period
- **Definition:** The length of time from the start of the policy, or of an added benefit, during which no claim is paid.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DEFP, which runs from the start of each disability.
- **Related terms:** [DEFP](#defp) deferred period, [TERM](#term) policy term, [INC](#inc) inception, [FUN](#fun) funeral cover

## Measures

Tokens for the MEASURE slot. They say what kind of number the variable holds.

### AMT

- **Full term:** Amount
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** value, money amount
- **Definition:** A money amount for the item, or the natural unit of the item. This is the implied measure and is never written in a name.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CNT. If no measure token is present the variable is an amount.
- **Related terms:** [CNT](#cnt) count, [RATE](#rate) rate, [PV](#pv) present value, [PER](#per) per

### CNT

- **Full term:** Count
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** number of
- **Definition:** The number of units of the item, such as policies, lives, events or units. Counts may be fractional in a projection because they are expected values.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PROB. A count scales with the size of the business. A probability lies between 0 and 1.
- **Related terms:** [AMT](#amt) amount, [PROB](#prob) probability, [POL](#pol) policy, [LIFE](#life) life assured, [PER](#per) per

### RATE

- **Full term:** Rate
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** qx, incidence rate, interest rate
- **Definition:** For an event, the probability that it happens within the stated time base to a policy exposed at the start. For interest, inflation or growth, the effective rate over the stated time base. A rate must always state its time base.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PROB (no time base). Not FORCE (instantaneous). Not FAC (a multiplier). Not a PER ratio (one item divided by another).
- **Related terms:** [PROB](#prob) probability, [FORCE](#force) force, [FAC](#fac) factor, [PER](#per) per, [ANN](#ann) annual, [MTH](#mth) monthly, [PRD](#prd) per model period, [DEP](#dep) dependent, [INDP](#indp) independent, [AMT](#amt) amount, [IDX](#idx) index, [INFL](#infl) inflation

### PROB

- **Full term:** Probability
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** survival probability, in-force factor
- **Definition:** The probability of being in a stated state at a stated time point, or of an outcome that has no time base.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RATE, which is the probability of an event over a time base. Not CNT.
- **Related terms:** [RATE](#rate) rate, [CNT](#cnt) count, [IF](#if) in force, [PERS](#pers) persistency, [FORCE](#force) force, [IND](#ind) indicator

### FORCE

- **Full term:** Force
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** instantaneous rate, hazard rate, transition intensity
- **Definition:** The instantaneous annual rate at which an event happens or interest accrues, as in the force of mortality or the force of interest.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RATE. A force can exceed 1 and is not a probability.
- **Related terms:** [RATE](#rate) rate, [PROB](#prob) probability, [DISC](#disc) discount

### PV

- **Full term:** Present value
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** discounted value, EPV
- **Definition:** The value at the stated time point of all amounts of the item due after that point, discounted and weighted by the probability of payment. A time point is required.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FUT (future amounts summed without discounting). Not ACC (past amounts rolled forward). Not BEL, which is a specific net present value defined by a reporting basis.
- **Related terms:** [FUT](#fut) future total, [ACC](#acc) accumulated, [CUM](#cum) cumulative, [DISC](#disc) discount, [AMT](#amt) amount, [BEL](#bel) best estimate liability

### FAC

- **Full term:** Factor
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** multiplier, scalar
- **Definition:** A number with no units that other values are multiplied by.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RATE. A discount rate of 5% and a discount factor of 0.95 are different variables. Not a PER ratio. Not facultative reinsurance.
- **Related terms:** [RATE](#rate) rate, [PER](#per) per, [IDX](#idx) index, [ADJ](#adj) adjustment, [DISC](#disc) discount, [ANTY](#anty) annuity

### IND

- **Full term:** Indicator
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** 0/1 variable, boolean
- **Definition:** A value of 1 when a stated condition holds at that time and 0 when it does not. It is calculated by the model and can change through the projection.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FLG, which is a setting chosen for the run. Not INDP (independent). Not IDX (index).
- **Related terms:** [FLG](#flg) flag, [PROB](#prob) probability, [ONER](#oner) onerous

### FLG

- **Full term:** Flag
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** switch, option, setting
- **Definition:** An input setting, fixed for the whole run, that turns a model feature on or off or selects between methods.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not IND, which the model calculates and which can change with time.
- **Related terms:** [IND](#ind) indicator

### IDX

- **Full term:** Index
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** index level
- **Definition:** The level of an index at the stated time point, relative to a base level of 1 at a stated origin.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RATE. The index is the accumulated effect of the rates. Not IND. Not an array subscript.
- **Related terms:** [RATE](#rate) rate, [FAC](#fac) factor, [INFL](#infl) inflation, [ESC](#esc) escalation

### PER

- **Full term:** Per
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** divided by, as a proportion of, per unit of
- **Definition:** Builds a ratio. It is always followed by exactly one item token, which is the denominator.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PRD (per model period). Not PERS (persistency). Not RATE: a PER ratio has an item as its base, a RATE has time as its base.
- **Related terms:** [RATE](#rate) rate, [FAC](#fac) factor, [AMT](#amt) amount, [CNT](#cnt) count, [POL](#pol) policy

### CUM

- **Full term:** Cumulative
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** to date, running total
- **Definition:** The simple sum of the item over all periods up to the stated time point, with no interest. A time point is required.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ACC, which adds interest. Not FUT, which looks forward.
- **Related terms:** [ACC](#acc) accumulated, [FUT](#fut) future total, [PV](#pv) present value, [AVG](#avg) average

### FUT

- **Full term:** Future total
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** remaining, undiscounted future
- **Definition:** The simple sum of the item over all periods after the stated time point, with no discounting. A time point is required.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PV, which discounts. Not REM, which narrows a contractual length. Not CUM, which looks back.
- **Related terms:** [PV](#pv) present value, [CUM](#cum) cumulative, [ACC](#acc) accumulated, [REM](#rem) remaining, [CU](#cu) coverage units

### ACC

- **Full term:** Accumulated
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** rolled up, with interest
- **Definition:** The sum of the item over all periods up to the stated time point, with interest added. A time point is required.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CUM (no interest). Not ACCD (accident). Not ACCR (interest accreted on the CSM).
- **Related terms:** [CUM](#cum) cumulative, [PV](#pv) present value, [FUT](#fut) future total, [INT](#int) interest, [ACCD](#accd) accident, [ACCR](#accr) interest accretion

### AVG

- **Full term:** Average
- **Slot:** MEASURE
- **Also called:** mean
- **Definition:** The mean of the item over the model period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not MOP, which is a single instant.
- **Related terms:** [MOP](#mop) middle of period, [CUM](#cum) cumulative

## Party

Tokens for the PARTY slot. They say whose share of the item the variable holds.

### GRS

- **Full term:** Gross of reinsurance
- **Slot:** PARTY
- **Also called:** direct, before reinsurance
- **Definition:** The whole of the item on the insurer's direct business before any reinsurance. This is the implied party and is never written in a name.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not the "gross premium" that means the premium charged to the policyholder, which is OFF. Not gross of tax, which uses BEF with TAX.
- **Related terms:** [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded, [NET](#net) net of reinsurance, [OFF](#off) office premium

### RI

- **Full term:** Reinsurance ceded
- **Slot:** PARTY
- **Also called:** reinsurers' share, reinsurance held, ceded
- **Definition:** The part of the item that passes between the insurer and its reinsurers under reinsurance the insurer has bought.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RINST (reinstatement). Not reinsurance accepted, which is direct business for the accepting insurer. Not RISK.
- **Related terms:** [GRS](#grs) gross of reinsurance, [NET](#net) net of reinsurance, [RET](#ret) retention, [QS](#qs) quota share, [XL](#xl) excess of loss, [LOREC](#lorec) loss-recovery component, [CPTY](#cpty) counterparty default risk, [FACL](#facl) facultative, [TRTY](#trty) treaty, [COINS](#coins) coinsurance

### NET

- **Full term:** Net of reinsurance
- **Slot:** PARTY
- **Also called:** retained, after reinsurance
- **Definition:** The item gross of reinsurance less the reinsurance ceded part.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not net of tax, which uses AFT with TAX. Not NCF (net cash flow). Not a net premium in the valuation sense, which this version does not cover.
- **Related terms:** [GRS](#grs) gross of reinsurance, [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded, [RET](#ret) retention, [NCF](#ncf) net cash flow, [NETP](#netp) net premium

### SH

- **Full term:** Shareholder
- **Slot:** PARTY
- **Also called:** shareholders' share, proprietor
- **Definition:** The part of the item that belongs to or is borne by shareholders.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PH. Tax paid on behalf of policyholders is not shareholder tax.
- **Related terms:** [PH](#ph) policyholder, [TRF](#trf) transfer, [TAX](#tax) tax, [PROF](#prof) profit, [CORF](#corf) corporate fund

### PH

- **Full term:** Policyholder
- **Slot:** PARTY
- **Also called:** policyholders' share
- **Definition:** The part of the item that belongs to or is borne by policyholders.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not POL, which is the policy as a unit of count. Not SH.
- **Related terms:** [SH](#sh) shareholder, [TAX](#tax) tax, [BON](#bon) bonus, [ASHR](#ashr) asset share, [POL](#pol) policy, [WP](#wp) with-profits, [IPF](#ipf) individual policyholder fund

## Basis

Tokens for the BASIS slot. They say which set of assumptions and rules the value is calculated on.

### BE

- **Full term:** Best estimate
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Also called:** central estimate, realistic, expected
- **Definition:** Assumptions set at the expected value with no margin for prudence or for risk.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not BEL, which is a liability calculated on best estimate assumptions under a reporting framework. Not PRU.
- **Related terms:** [PRU](#pru) prudent, [PRC](#prc) pricing, [MGN](#mgn) margin, [BEL](#bel) best estimate liability, [IFC](#ifc) IFRS 17 current, [IFL](#ifl) IFRS 17 locked-in

### PRU

- **Full term:** Prudent
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Also called:** with margins, conservative, reserving basis
- **Definition:** Assumptions that include deliberate margins above best estimate.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PRC. A pricing basis may or may not be prudent.
- **Related terms:** [BE](#be) best estimate, [MGN](#mgn) margin, [PRC](#prc) pricing

### PRC

- **Full term:** Pricing
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Also called:** premium basis
- **Definition:** The assumptions used to set premiums or charges for the product.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not UPRC (unit price). Not PRU.
- **Related terms:** [BE](#be) best estimate, [PRU](#pru) prudent, [LOAD](#load) loading, [OFF](#off) office premium

### IFL

- **Full term:** IFRS 17 locked-in
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Also called:** locked-in rates, rates at initial recognition
- **Definition:** The IFRS 17 basis that uses discount rates fixed at the initial recognition of the group of contracts. It is used for interest on the CSM and for adjustments to the CSM under the general model, and for finance expense in profit or loss when the other comprehensive income option is chosen.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not IFC. Not IF (in force). Fulfilment cash flows on the balance sheet are always at current rates, so a present value at locked-in rates is a second measurement and never the carrying amount.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs B72, B96 and B131
- **Related terms:** [IFC](#ifc) IFRS 17 current, [BE](#be) best estimate, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [ACCR](#accr) interest accretion, [IFE](#ife) insurance finance income or expenses, [OCI](#oci) other comprehensive income, [DISC](#disc) discount

### IFC

- **Full term:** IFRS 17 current
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Also called:** current rates
- **Definition:** The IFRS 17 basis that uses discount rates and assumptions current at the measurement date.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not IFL. Not IF (in force).
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 36 and B72(a)
- **Related terms:** [IFL](#ifl) IFRS 17 locked-in, [BE](#be) best estimate, [FCF](#fcf) fulfilment cash flows, [IFE](#ife) insurance finance income or expenses, [OCI](#oci) other comprehensive income, [DISC](#disc) discount

### SII

- **Full term:** Solvency II
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Definition:** The basis set by the European Solvency II rules for technical provisions and capital.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SAM, which is the South African regime and differs in detail.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35
- **Related terms:** [SAM](#sam) Solvency Assessment and Management, [TP](#tp) technical provisions, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [MCR](#mcr) minimum capital requirement, [OF](#of) own funds, [RM](#rm) risk margin

### EV

- **Full term:** Embedded value
- **Slot:** ITEM or BASIS
- **Definition:** As an item, the value of shareholders' interests in the covered business: adjusted net worth plus the value of in-force less the cost of required capital. It excludes future new business. As a basis, the assumptions and rules used to calculate it.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not VIF, which is one part of it. Not VNB, which covers new business only. EEV and MCEV are other embedded value frameworks with their own rules, so a description must say which framework applies.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 107 version 8 sections 2.1 and 2.3
- **Related terms:** [ANW](#anw) adjusted net worth, [VIF](#vif) value of in-force, [VNB](#vnb) value of new business, [COC](#coc) cost of capital, [TVOG](#tvog) time value of options and guarantees, [PROF](#prof) profit, [FSUR](#fsur) free surplus, [RCAP](#rcap) required capital

### RN

- **Full term:** Risk neutral
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Definition:** An economic basis in which all assets are expected to earn the risk-free rate, used to value options and guarantees.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RW. Not a statement about how prudent the non-economic assumptions are. Not the same as market consistent, which means reproducing market prices and can also be done with real world scenarios and deflators.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 110 version 5 appendices 2 and 3
- **Related terms:** [RW](#rw) real world, [TVOG](#tvog) time value of options and guarantees, [GTEE](#gtee) guarantee, [DISC](#disc) discount

### RW

- **Full term:** Real world
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Definition:** An economic basis in which assets are expected to earn their realistic returns including risk premiums.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RN. Not BE: a real world economic basis can be paired with any non-economic basis.
- **Related terms:** [RN](#rn) risk neutral, [INVR](#invr) investment return

## Decrements and policy status

Events that change the state of a policy, and the states themselves.

### DEC

- **Full term:** Decrement
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** exit cause, mode of exit
- **Definition:** Any event that removes a policy from a state in the model. It is the umbrella term for all causes together.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not EXIT. A decrement can move a policy to another state such as paid-up without ending it. EXIT always ends the policy.
- **Related terms:** [EXIT](#exit) exit, [DTH](#dth) death, [LPS](#lps) lapse, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [MAT](#mat) maturity, [EXPY](#expy) expiry, [PUP](#pup) paid-up, [DIS](#dis) disability, [DEP](#dep) dependent, [INDP](#indp) independent, [DCON](#dcon) discontinuance

### EXIT

- **Full term:** Exit
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** termination, policy ending, off
- **Definition:** Any event that ends the policy, whatever the cause: death, lapse, surrender, maturity, expiry or cancellation.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DEC, which also covers changes of state where the policy continues. Not EXPY, which is one kind of exit.
- **Related terms:** [DEC](#dec) decrement, [DTH](#dth) death, [LPS](#lps) lapse, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [MAT](#mat) maturity, [EXPY](#expy) expiry, [NTU](#ntu) not taken up, [DCON](#dcon) discontinuance, [IF](#if) in force, [PERS](#pers) persistency

### DTH

- **Full term:** Death
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** mortality
- **Definition:** The death of the life assured. As an item it is the event. As a kind it marks a claim or benefit triggered by death.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not MORT, which is the capital risk that death rates turn out higher than expected. A mortality rate is the rate of death and uses DTH.
- **Related terms:** [MORT](#mort) mortality risk, [LONG](#long) longevity risk, [DEC](#dec) decrement, [EXIT](#exit) exit, [ACCD](#accd) accident, [CI](#ci) critical illness, [DIS](#dis) disability, [SAR](#sar) sum at risk, [LIFE](#life) life assured, [SEL](#sel) select, [ULT](#ult) ultimate, [TBL](#tbl) table, [IMP](#imp) improvement, [EXTRA](#extra) extra, [CLM](#clm) claim, [ACEL](#acel) accelerated, [FUN](#fun) funeral cover, [HIV](#hiv) HIV and AIDS, [CRDL](#crdl) credit life

### LPS

- **Full term:** Lapse
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** forfeiture
- **Definition:** The policy ends because premiums due were not paid, and no value is paid to the policyholder.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SUR, where a value is paid. Not PUP, where the policy continues without premiums. Not WDL, where the policy continues. Not NTU, where the policy is cancelled from the start. Not LPSR, the capital risk, which covers far more than lapse.
- **Source checked:** SAP 104 version 9 section 6.8 (ASSA, withdrawn 2023) treats lapse as the case with no surrender value
- **Related terms:** [SUR](#sur) surrender, [WDL](#wdl) partial withdrawal, [PUP](#pup) paid-up, [RINST](#rinst) reinstatement, [PERS](#pers) persistency, [DCON](#dcon) discontinuance, [NTU](#ntu) not taken up, [EXIT](#exit) exit, [DEC](#dec) decrement, [EXPY](#expy) expiry, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk, [CLAW](#claw) clawback, [MASS](#mass) mass

### SUR

- **Full term:** Surrender
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** cash-in, encashment, full withdrawal
- **Definition:** The policyholder chooses to end the policy before its term and a surrender value is paid.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LPS, where nothing is paid. Not WDL, where only part of the value is taken and the policy continues. Not MAT, which is the scheduled end. Not SV, which is the amount and not the event.
- **Source checked:** SAP 104 version 9 section 6.8 (ASSA, withdrawn 2023)
- **Related terms:** [LPS](#lps) lapse, [WDL](#wdl) partial withdrawal, [PUP](#pup) paid-up, [DCON](#dcon) discontinuance, [SV](#sv) surrender value, [MAT](#mat) maturity, [EXIT](#exit) exit, [DEC](#dec) decrement, [PERS](#pers) persistency, [CLM](#clm) claim, [MVR](#mvr) market value reduction, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk

### WDL

- **Full term:** Partial withdrawal
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** partial surrender, partial encashment
- **Definition:** The policyholder takes part of the policy value and the policy continues in force. In this standard withdrawal always means partial withdrawal.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SUR, which ends the policy. Not the umbrella sense of withdrawal as any voluntary exit, which is DCON. Not regular income from an annuity, which is ANTY.
- **Related terms:** [SUR](#sur) surrender, [LPS](#lps) lapse, [DCON](#dcon) discontinuance, [FV](#fv) fund value, [SV](#sv) surrender value, [MVR](#mvr) market value reduction, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk

### DCON

- **Full term:** Discontinuance
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** voluntary termination, withdrawal in the umbrella sense, termination (older South African guidance)
- **Definition:** The policy ends through the policyholder's action or inaction. It is lapse and surrender taken together.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not WDL. Not DISC (discount). Not PUP: a paid-up policy has stopped premiums but has not ended. Not EXIT, which includes death and maturity.
- **Related terms:** [LPS](#lps) lapse, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [WDL](#wdl) partial withdrawal, [PUP](#pup) paid-up, [EXIT](#exit) exit, [PERS](#pers) persistency, [DEC](#dec) decrement, [NTU](#ntu) not taken up, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk

### PUP

- **Full term:** Paid-up
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** premium cessation, reduced paid-up, made paid-up
- **Definition:** Premiums stop before the end of the premium term and the policy continues, usually with reduced benefits. As an item it is the event. As a kind it is the status.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LPS or SUR, where the policy ends. Not a policy whose premium term has finished as scheduled. Not WAIV, where premiums stop because a benefit pays them.
- **Related terms:** [LPS](#lps) lapse, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [DCON](#dcon) discontinuance, [RINST](#rinst) reinstatement, [PERS](#pers) persistency, [IF](#if) in force, [POL](#pol) policy, [WAIV](#waiv) waiver of premium, [DEC](#dec) decrement, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk

### RINST

- **Full term:** Reinstatement
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** revival, reactivation
- **Definition:** A policy that lapsed or became paid-up is restored to premium-paying in-force status.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RI (reinsurance). Not NB: a reinstated policy is not new business. Not REC, which is recovery of a disabled life.
- **Related terms:** [LPS](#lps) lapse, [PUP](#pup) paid-up, [PERS](#pers) persistency, [NB](#nb) new business, [IF](#if) in force, [REC](#rec) recovery

### PERS

- **Full term:** Persistency
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** retention, continuation
- **Definition:** The proportion of policies, or of premium, that is still in force at the stated time point out of those that started. The description must say whether it counts policies or premium.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PROB of being in force, which also allows for death and maturity unless stated. Not PRD or PER.
- **Related terms:** [LPS](#lps) lapse, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [DCON](#dcon) discontinuance, [PUP](#pup) paid-up, [RINST](#rinst) reinstatement, [IF](#if) in force, [EXIT](#exit) exit, [PROB](#prob) probability, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk

### MAT

- **Full term:** Maturity
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** endowment maturity, survival to term
- **Definition:** The policy reaches the end of its term and a maturity benefit is paid.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not EXPY, where the term ends and nothing is paid. Not SUR, which happens before the end of the term. Not MTH or MAINT.
- **Related terms:** [EXPY](#expy) expiry, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [TERM](#term) policy term, [EXIT](#exit) exit, [DEC](#dec) decrement, [GTEE](#gtee) guarantee, [CLM](#clm) claim

### EXPY

- **Full term:** Expiry
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** end of cover, run-off
- **Definition:** The policy reaches the end of its term and cover ends with no benefit paid.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not MAT, where a benefit is paid. Not LPS, which is early and caused by non-payment. Not EXP (expense) or EXPV.
- **Related terms:** [MAT](#mat) maturity, [LPS](#lps) lapse, [TERM](#term) policy term, [EXIT](#exit) exit, [DEC](#dec) decrement

### NTU

- **Full term:** Not taken up
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** cancelled from inception
- **Definition:** A policy is cancelled from inception, before or shortly after it starts, and any premiums paid are refunded.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LPS. A not taken up policy is treated as if it never started. A lapsed policy was in force for a time. Not the same as a cooling-off cancellation under South African rules, where the refund can be reduced for cover already given.
- **Source checked:** Not a defined term in the sources checked. Cooling-off: Policyholder Protection Rules (Long-term Insurance), rule 4
- **Related terms:** [LPS](#lps) lapse, [DCON](#dcon) discontinuance, [EXIT](#exit) exit, [NB](#nb) new business, [CLAW](#claw) clawback

### DIS

- **Full term:** Disability
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** incapacity, disablement
- **Definition:** The life assured becomes unable to work or to perform defined activities under the policy definition. As an item it is the event of becoming disabled.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DISC (discount) or DISTE or DIV. Not CI, which pays on diagnosis of a listed condition whatever the ability to work. Not MORB, which is the capital risk.
- **Related terms:** [REC](#rec) recovery, [CI](#ci) critical illness, [MORB](#morb) morbidity risk, [INCM](#incm) income benefit, [LS](#ls) lump sum, [WAIV](#waiv) waiver of premium, [DTH](#dth) death, [ACCD](#accd) accident, [DEC](#dec) decrement, [DEFP](#defp) deferred period, [CLM](#clm) claim, [ACEL](#acel) accelerated, [SALN](#saln) stand-alone, [CRDL](#crdl) credit life

### REC

- **Full term:** Recovery
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** return to work, claim termination by recovery
- **Definition:** A disabled life ceases to be disabled under the policy definition and benefit payments stop.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a reinsurance recovery, which is a claim with party RI. Not RINST.
- **Related terms:** [DIS](#dis) disability, [INCM](#incm) income benefit, [RINST](#rinst) reinstatement, [MORB](#morb) morbidity risk

### CI

- **Full term:** Critical illness
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** dread disease, severe illness, trauma cover
- **Definition:** The life assured is diagnosed with one of the conditions listed in the policy, which triggers a benefit.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DIS, which depends on ability to work. Not CU (coverage units).
- **Source checked:** SAP 104 version 9 section 6.10 (ASSA, withdrawn 2023) uses the name dread disease
- **Related terms:** [DIS](#dis) disability, [DTH](#dth) death, [MORB](#morb) morbidity risk, [LS](#ls) lump sum, [ACCD](#accd) accident, [CLM](#clm) claim, [ACEL](#acel) accelerated, [SALN](#saln) stand-alone

### ACCD

- **Full term:** Accident
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** accidental
- **Definition:** Narrows an event or benefit to cases caused by accident as defined in the policy.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ACC (accumulated). Not ACCR.
- **Related terms:** [DTH](#dth) death, [DIS](#dis) disability, [CI](#ci) critical illness, [ACC](#acc) accumulated

### IF

- **Full term:** In force
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** active, on the books
- **Definition:** The status of a policy that has started and has not ended. Paid-up policies are in force.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not IFL or IFC, which are IFRS 17 bases. Not premium paying: a description that means premium-paying policies only must say so.
- **Related terms:** [POL](#pol) policy, [LIFE](#life) life assured, [PUP](#pup) paid-up, [NB](#nb) new business, [EXIT](#exit) exit, [PERS](#pers) persistency, [PROB](#prob) probability, [RINST](#rinst) reinstatement

### POL

- **Full term:** Policy
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** contract
- **Definition:** One insurance contract as the unit of count. Amounts per policy use PER with POL.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PH (policyholder as a party). Not LIFE: one policy can cover several lives and one life can hold several policies.
- **Related terms:** [LIFE](#life) life assured, [IF](#if) in force, [NB](#nb) new business, [CNT](#cnt) count, [PER](#per) per, [PH](#ph) policyholder, [PUP](#pup) paid-up

### LIFE

- **Full term:** Life assured
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** insured life, member
- **Definition:** One insured person as the unit of count.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not POL. On joint life and group business the two counts differ.
- **Related terms:** [POL](#pol) policy, [IF](#if) in force, [CNT](#cnt) count, [AGE](#age) age, [DTH](#dth) death, [FUN](#fun) funeral cover

## Assumption building

Tokens that describe how a rate or assumption is built up.

### DEP

- **Full term:** Dependent
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** multiple decrement rate
- **Definition:** Narrows a decrement rate to the rate that applies when all other decrements are operating at the same time.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INDP. Not a dependant person. A dependent rate is never larger than the matching independent rate.
- **Related terms:** [INDP](#indp) independent, [RATE](#rate) rate, [DEC](#dec) decrement

### INDP

- **Full term:** Independent
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** single decrement rate, absolute rate
- **Definition:** Narrows a decrement rate to the rate that would apply if this decrement were the only one operating.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not IND (indicator). Not DEP.
- **Related terms:** [DEP](#dep) dependent, [RATE](#rate) rate, [DEC](#dec) decrement, [TBL](#tbl) table

### SEL

- **Full term:** Select
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** select period
- **Definition:** Narrows a rate to the period shortly after underwriting, when rates depend on policy duration as well as age.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ULT. Not an option selected by a user, which is a FLG.
- **Related terms:** [ULT](#ult) ultimate, [TBL](#tbl) table, [DTH](#dth) death

### ULT

- **Full term:** Ultimate
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows a rate to the period after the select period, when rates depend on age only.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SEL. Not the ultimate cost of claims in a reserving sense.
- **Related terms:** [SEL](#sel) select, [TBL](#tbl) table, [DTH](#dth) death

### TBL

- **Full term:** Table
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** base table, standard table
- **Definition:** Narrows a rate to the value read from a published or company base table, before any adjustment.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not the adjusted rate the model finally uses, which carries no TBL token.
- **Related terms:** [ADJ](#adj) adjustment, [SEL](#sel) select, [ULT](#ult) ultimate, [IMP](#imp) improvement, [DTH](#dth) death, [INDP](#indp) independent

### ADJ

- **Full term:** Adjustment
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** scaling, experience adjustment to a table
- **Definition:** Narrows a factor or amount to one that adjusts a base value towards the assumption wanted, such as a percentage of a table.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not EXPV (difference between actual and expected). Not LOAD, which is added for a stated purpose. Not MGN, which is added for prudence.
- **Related terms:** [TBL](#tbl) table, [FAC](#fac) factor, [LOAD](#load) loading, [MGN](#mgn) margin, [IMP](#imp) improvement, [EXPV](#expv) experience variance

### IMP

- **Full term:** Improvement
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** mortality improvement, trend
- **Definition:** Narrows a rate or factor to the expected change in a decrement rate from one calendar year to the next.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not impairment of assets. Not ADJ, which is a level change and not a trend over calendar time.
- **Related terms:** [TBL](#tbl) table, [ADJ](#adj) adjustment, [DTH](#dth) death, [LONG](#long) longevity risk

### LOAD

- **Full term:** Loading
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** extra, addition
- **Definition:** An addition to a base rate, premium or charge for a stated purpose, such as an extra mortality loading or an expense loading in a premium.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not MGN, which is added for prudence and released if not needed. Not ADJ, which rescales a base to best estimate.
- **Related terms:** [MGN](#mgn) margin, [ADJ](#adj) adjustment, [PRC](#prc) pricing, [OFF](#off) office premium, [RISK](#risk) risk premium, [EXTRA](#extra) extra, [HIV](#hiv) HIV and AIDS

### MGN

- **Full term:** Margin
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** prudential margin, provision for adverse deviation
- **Definition:** The deliberate difference between a prudent assumption and the best estimate assumption.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LOAD. Not a profit margin, which is a PER ratio of profit to premium. Not RM or RA, which are liabilities.
- **Source checked:** ASSA SAP 104 version 9 section 3.3
- **Related terms:** [LOAD](#load) loading, [ADJ](#adj) adjustment, [BE](#be) best estimate, [PRU](#pru) prudent, [RA](#ra) risk adjustment, [RM](#rm) risk margin, [COMP](#comp) compulsory, [DISCR](#discr) discretionary, [FSV](#fsv) financial soundness valuation

### SHK

- **Full term:** Shock
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** stress size
- **Definition:** The size of the change applied to an assumption in a stress test or capital calculation.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not the result of the stress, which is a capital amount such as SCR. Not EXPV.
- **Related terms:** [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [UP](#up) up, [DOWN](#down) down, [MASS](#mass) mass, [MORT](#mort) mortality risk, [CAT](#cat) catastrophe risk, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk, [REVN](#revn) revision risk

## Cash flows in general

Items that describe flows of money without saying what kind.

### CF

- **Full term:** Cash flow
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** Any amount of money that moves into or out of the insurer in the model period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not NCF, which is the total of inflows less outflows. Not FCF, which is a present value.
- **Related terms:** [NCF](#ncf) net cash flow, [PREM](#prem) premium, [CLM](#clm) claim, [EXP](#exp) expense, [COMM](#comm) commission, [TAX](#tax) tax, [FCF](#fcf) fulfilment cash flows

### NCF

- **Full term:** Net cash flow
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** net income
- **Definition:** Total cash inflows less total cash outflows in the model period, before any change in reserves.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not NET (net of reinsurance). Not PROF, which also allows for changes in reserves.
- **Related terms:** [CF](#cf) cash flow, [PROF](#prof) profit, [NET](#net) net of reinsurance, [TRF](#trf) transfer

### TAX

- **Full term:** Tax
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Definition:** Tax payable by the insurer. The description must say on what and for whose account.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not deferred tax, which this version does not cover. Profit before or after tax uses BEF or AFT with TAX.
- **Related terms:** [SH](#sh) shareholder, [PH](#ph) policyholder, [PROF](#prof) profit, [CF](#cf) cash flow, [INVI](#invi) investment income, [LAC](#lac) loss-absorbing capacity, [IPF](#ipf) individual policyholder fund, [CPF](#cpf) company policyholder fund, [UPF](#upf) untaxed policyholder fund, [RPF](#rpf) risk policy fund, [CORF](#corf) corporate fund

## Premiums

Premium items and the tokens that narrow them.

### PREM

- **Full term:** Premium
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** contribution, consideration
- **Definition:** The amount payable by the policyholder to the insurer for cover or investment in the model period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CHRG, which is deducted from a fund and not paid in. Not a reinsurance premium unless party RI is stated.
- **Related terms:** [SGL](#sgl) single, [REG](#reg) regular, [OFF](#off) office premium, [RISK](#risk) risk premium, [WAIV](#waiv) waiver of premium, [ESC](#esc) escalation, [ALLOC](#alloc) allocation, [APE](#ape) annual premium equivalent, [CHRG](#chrg) charge, [CF](#cf) cash flow, [EXTRA](#extra) extra, [NETP](#netp) net premium, [POLF](#polf) policy fee, [UPR](#upr) unearned premium reserve

### SGL

- **Full term:** Single
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** lump sum premium, once-off
- **Definition:** Narrows a premium to one paid once with no commitment to pay again.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not REG. Not LS, which is a lump sum benefit.
- **Related terms:** [REG](#reg) regular, [PREM](#prem) premium, [APE](#ape) annual premium equivalent

### REG

- **Full term:** Regular
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** recurring, periodic
- **Definition:** Narrows a premium to one payable at regular intervals over a premium term.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not REN. A regular premium includes the first one. Renewal refers only to those after the first period.
- **Related terms:** [SGL](#sgl) single, [REN](#ren) renewal, [PREM](#prem) premium, [APE](#ape) annual premium equivalent, [ESC](#esc) escalation

### OFF

- **Full term:** Office premium
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** gross premium, tariff premium, charged premium
- **Definition:** Narrows a premium to the full amount charged to the policyholder, including all loadings for expenses, commission and profit.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not GRS. "Gross premium" can mean this or can mean gross of reinsurance, so this standard does not use the word gross for either without saying which.
- **Related terms:** [RISK](#risk) risk premium, [PREM](#prem) premium, [LOAD](#load) loading, [GRS](#grs) gross of reinsurance, [PRC](#prc) pricing, [EXTRA](#extra) extra, [NETP](#netp) net premium

### RISK

- **Full term:** Risk premium
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** cost of cover, pure risk premium
- **Definition:** Narrows a premium or charge to the part that covers the expected cost of claims in the period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a capital risk such as MORT. Not RI. Not a risk premium in the investment sense of extra expected return.
- **Related terms:** [OFF](#off) office premium, [PREM](#prem) premium, [CHRG](#chrg) charge, [SAR](#sar) sum at risk, [LOAD](#load) loading, [NETP](#netp) net premium, [COINS](#coins) coinsurance, [RPF](#rpf) risk policy fund

### WAIV

- **Full term:** Waiver of premium
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** premium waiver, premium protection
- **Definition:** Narrows a benefit or claim to one that pays the policy's premiums while the life assured is disabled or after another stated event.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PUP. Under waiver the premiums are still credited to the policy. Under paid-up they stop.
- **Related terms:** [DIS](#dis) disability, [PUP](#pup) paid-up, [PREM](#prem) premium, [CLM](#clm) claim

### ESC

- **Full term:** Escalation
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** indexation, automatic increase
- **Definition:** The scheduled contractual increase in a premium or benefit from year to year.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INFL, which is an economic assumption and not a policy term. Not BON, which is added at the insurer's discretion.
- **Related terms:** [INFL](#infl) inflation, [IDX](#idx) index, [PREM](#prem) premium, [BEN](#ben) benefit, [REG](#reg) regular, [BON](#bon) bonus

### EXTRA

- **Full term:** Extra
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** extra premium, extra risk, substandard, rated
- **Definition:** Narrows a premium or a rate to the addition made because the life is not acceptable at standard rates.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LOAD in general. An extra is a loading for one cause only: a risk that is worse than standard.
- **Related terms:** [LOAD](#load) loading, [PREM](#prem) premium, [OFF](#off) office premium, [DTH](#dth) death

### NETP

- **Full term:** Net premium
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** valuation net premium
- **Definition:** Narrows a premium to the one that would just provide the contractual benefits on the valuation basis, with no allowance for expenses or profit.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not NET, which is net of reinsurance. Not RISK, which is the cost of cover for one period.
- **Related terms:** [OFF](#off) office premium, [RISK](#risk) risk premium, [PREM](#prem) premium, [NET](#net) net of reinsurance, [RES](#res) reserve

## Claims and benefits

What the insurer pays under the contract and the levels of cover.

### CLM

- **Full term:** Claim
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** benefit payment, policy outgo
- **Definition:** The amount the insurer pays under the contract in the model period when a contractual event happens. In a projection it is the expected amount.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not BEN, which is the amount that would be payable if the event happened. A surrender or maturity payment is a claim in this standard.
- **Related terms:** [BEN](#ben) benefit, [SA](#sa) sum assured, [DTH](#dth) death, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [MAT](#mat) maturity, [DIS](#dis) disability, [CI](#ci) critical illness, [ANTY](#anty) annuity, [WAIV](#waiv) waiver of premium, [LS](#ls) lump sum, [INCM](#incm) income benefit, [CF](#cf) cash flow, [INVC](#invc) investment component, [OCR](#ocr) outstanding claims reserve, [IBNR](#ibnr) incurred but not reported, [FUN](#fun) funeral cover, [RETR](#retr) retrenchment

### BEN

- **Full term:** Benefit
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** cover amount, benefit level
- **Definition:** The amount per policy that would be payable if the stated event happened at the stated time point.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CLM, which is the expected payment. Not SA, which is the basic contractual amount before bonuses and other additions.
- **Related terms:** [CLM](#clm) claim, [SA](#sa) sum assured, [SAR](#sar) sum at risk, [SV](#sv) surrender value, [BON](#bon) bonus, [GTEE](#gtee) guarantee, [ESC](#esc) escalation, [LS](#ls) lump sum, [INCM](#incm) income benefit, [ACEL](#acel) accelerated, [SALN](#saln) stand-alone

### SA

- **Full term:** Sum assured
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** sum insured, face amount, cover
- **Definition:** The basic amount stated in the policy as payable on the main insured event, before any bonuses.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SAR, which deducts the reserve or fund held. Not BEN, which includes additions. Not SAM or SV.
- **Related terms:** [SAR](#sar) sum at risk, [BEN](#ben) benefit, [CLM](#clm) claim, [BON](#bon) bonus, [RET](#ret) retention, [QS](#qs) quota share

### SAR

- **Full term:** Sum at risk
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** net amount at risk, death strain at risk
- **Definition:** The benefit payable on the insured event less the reserve or fund held for the policy at that time.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SA. The description must say which reserve or fund is deducted. Not SCR.
- **Related terms:** [SA](#sa) sum assured, [BEN](#ben) benefit, [RES](#res) reserve, [FV](#fv) fund value, [RISK](#risk) risk premium, [DTH](#dth) death, [RET](#ret) retention

### ANTY

- **Full term:** Annuity
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** pension in payment, income stream
- **Definition:** A series of payments made at regular intervals while a stated condition holds, usually the survival of a life.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ANN (annual). Not INCM, which is a disability income benefit. Not WDL.
- **Related terms:** [CLM](#clm) claim, [INCM](#incm) income benefit, [LONG](#long) longevity risk, [FAC](#fac) factor, [GTEE](#gtee) guarantee, [REVN](#revn) revision risk, [UPF](#upf) untaxed policyholder fund

### LS

- **Full term:** Lump sum
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** capital benefit
- **Definition:** Narrows a benefit or claim to one paid as a single amount.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INCM. Not SGL, which is a single premium.
- **Related terms:** [INCM](#incm) income benefit, [CLM](#clm) claim, [BEN](#ben) benefit, [DIS](#dis) disability, [CI](#ci) critical illness, [FUN](#fun) funeral cover

### INCM

- **Full term:** Income benefit
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** income protection, permanent health insurance, disability income
- **Definition:** Narrows a benefit or claim to one paid as regular instalments while the insured condition lasts.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INC (inception). Not INVI (investment income). Not ANTY, which is paid on survival and not on disability.
- **Related terms:** [LS](#ls) lump sum, [DIS](#dis) disability, [REC](#rec) recovery, [ANTY](#anty) annuity, [CLM](#clm) claim, [BEN](#ben) benefit, [DEFP](#defp) deferred period

### ACEL

- **Full term:** Accelerated
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** accelerated benefit, rider benefit
- **Definition:** Narrows a benefit or claim to one whose payment brings forward part or all of the death benefit and reduces it by the amount paid.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SALN, where the death benefit is not reduced.
- **Related terms:** [SALN](#saln) stand-alone, [CI](#ci) critical illness, [DIS](#dis) disability, [DTH](#dth) death, [BEN](#ben) benefit

### SALN

- **Full term:** Stand-alone
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** independent benefit
- **Definition:** Narrows a benefit or claim to one that is paid without reducing any other benefit under the policy.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ACEL.
- **Related terms:** [ACEL](#acel) accelerated, [CI](#ci) critical illness, [DIS](#dis) disability, [BEN](#ben) benefit

## Expenses and commission

The insurer's own costs and what it pays to intermediaries.

### EXP

- **Full term:** Expense
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** cost
- **Definition:** A cost the insurer incurs in running its business, other than claims, commission and tax.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not EXPY (expiry). Not EXPV (experience variance). Not COMM: commission is a separate item in this standard and is never included in EXP.
- **Related terms:** [COMM](#comm) commission, [INIT](#init) initial, [REN](#ren) renewal, [ACQ](#acq) acquisition, [MAINT](#maint) maintenance, [ATTR](#attr) directly attributable, [NATTR](#nattr) not directly attributable, [INV](#inv) investment, [INFL](#infl) inflation, [CF](#cf) cash flow, [IACF](#iacf) insurance acquisition cash flows, [ISE](#ise) insurance service expenses, [OH](#oh) overhead

### INIT

- **Full term:** Initial
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** first year, upfront
- **Definition:** Narrows an expense or commission to amounts that arise at or around the start of the policy. It is defined by timing.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ACQ, which is defined by purpose. Not INC, which is a time point. Not INT.
- **Related terms:** [REN](#ren) renewal, [ACQ](#acq) acquisition, [EXP](#exp) expense, [COMM](#comm) commission, [CLAW](#claw) clawback, [INC](#inc) inception

### REN

- **Full term:** Renewal
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** recurring, ongoing
- **Definition:** Narrows an expense, commission or premium to amounts that arise in periods after the first. It is defined by timing.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not MAINT, which is defined by purpose. Not REG. Not RINST. Not RN.
- **Related terms:** [INIT](#init) initial, [MAINT](#maint) maintenance, [REG](#reg) regular, [EXP](#exp) expense, [COMM](#comm) commission

### ACQ

- **Full term:** Acquisition
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** new business cost
- **Definition:** Narrows an expense to the cost of selling, underwriting and starting contracts. It is defined by purpose, and can arise after the first period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INIT, which is defined by timing. Not IACF, which is the IFRS 17 measure of acquisition cash flows including commission.
- **Related terms:** [INIT](#init) initial, [MAINT](#maint) maintenance, [IACF](#iacf) insurance acquisition cash flows, [EXP](#exp) expense, [NB](#nb) new business, [ATTR](#attr) directly attributable

### MAINT

- **Full term:** Maintenance
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** administration, servicing
- **Definition:** Narrows an expense to the cost of administering policies already in force. It is defined by purpose.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not REN, which is defined by timing. Not MAT.
- **Related terms:** [ACQ](#acq) acquisition, [REN](#ren) renewal, [EXP](#exp) expense, [INV](#inv) investment, [ATTR](#attr) directly attributable, [OH](#oh) overhead, [POLF](#polf) policy fee

### ATTR

- **Full term:** Directly attributable
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows an expense to costs that relate directly to fulfilling insurance contracts and so are included in IFRS 17 fulfilment cash flows.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not NATTR. Not a statement that the cost varies with volume.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraph B65
- **Related terms:** [NATTR](#nattr) not directly attributable, [EXP](#exp) expense, [FCF](#fcf) fulfilment cash flows, [ACQ](#acq) acquisition, [MAINT](#maint) maintenance, [ISE](#ise) insurance service expenses, [OH](#oh) overhead

### NATTR

- **Full term:** Not directly attributable
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** non-attributable
- **Definition:** Narrows an expense to costs that cannot be directly attributed to the portfolio of insurance contracts. They are left out of IFRS 17 fulfilment cash flows and are expensed when incurred.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ATTR. These costs are still real and still appear in profit.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraph B66(d)
- **Related terms:** [ATTR](#attr) directly attributable, [EXP](#exp) expense, [FCF](#fcf) fulfilment cash flows, [OH](#oh) overhead

### INV

- **Full term:** Investment
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** asset management
- **Definition:** Narrows an expense or other item to the part that relates to managing the assets.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INVI (the income earned). Not INVR (the total return). Not INVC (IFRS 17 investment component).
- **Related terms:** [INVI](#invi) investment income, [INVR](#invr) investment return, [INVC](#invc) investment component, [EXP](#exp) expense, [MAINT](#maint) maintenance, [ASSET](#asset) assets, [FMC](#fmc) fund management charge

### COMM

- **Full term:** Commission
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** brokerage, intermediary remuneration
- **Definition:** An amount paid to an intermediary for selling or servicing a policy. With party RI it is commission the reinsurer pays to the insurer.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not EXP. Not CHRG.
- **Related terms:** [EXP](#exp) expense, [INIT](#init) initial, [REN](#ren) renewal, [CLAW](#claw) clawback, [IACF](#iacf) insurance acquisition cash flows, [CF](#cf) cash flow

### CLAW

- **Full term:** Clawback
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** commission recovery
- **Definition:** Narrows commission to amounts recovered from the intermediary because the policy ended early.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a reinsurance recovery. Not NTU, which is one of the events that can trigger it.
- **Related terms:** [COMM](#comm) commission, [INIT](#init) initial, [LPS](#lps) lapse, [NTU](#ntu) not taken up

### INFL

- **Full term:** Inflation
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** price inflation, cost escalation
- **Definition:** The general rise in prices or costs assumed in the economic basis.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ESC, which is a contractual increase written into a policy.
- **Related terms:** [ESC](#esc) escalation, [IDX](#idx) index, [EXP](#exp) expense, [RATE](#rate) rate, [DISC](#disc) discount

### OH

- **Full term:** Overhead
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** overheads, fixed expenses
- **Definition:** Narrows an expense to costs that do not vary with the amount of business written or in force.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not NATTR. An overhead can still be directly attributable to fulfilling contracts.
- **Related terms:** [EXP](#exp) expense, [MAINT](#maint) maintenance, [ATTR](#attr) directly attributable, [NATTR](#nattr) not directly attributable, [POLF](#polf) policy fee

## Policy values, unit-linked and with-profits

Values attached to one policy and the features of savings business.

### FV

- **Full term:** Fund value
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** account value, account balance, unit fund
- **Definition:** The value of the policyholder's account or units at the stated time point, before any surrender charge.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SV, which is what would be paid on surrender. Not FSUR. Not a fair value.
- **Related terms:** [SV](#sv) surrender value, [UNIT](#unit) unit, [UPRC](#uprc) unit price, [CHRG](#chrg) charge, [ALLOC](#alloc) allocation, [SAR](#sar) sum at risk, [ASHR](#ashr) asset share, [WDL](#wdl) partial withdrawal, [NUNIT](#nunit) non-unit, [FMC](#fmc) fund management charge, [BID](#bid) bid, [UNDL](#undl) underlying items

### SV

- **Full term:** Surrender value
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** cash value, encashment value
- **Definition:** The amount per policy that would be paid if the policy were surrendered at the stated time point.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FV. Not the expected surrender payments in a period, which is CLM with kind SUR.
- **Related terms:** [FV](#fv) fund value, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [BEN](#ben) benefit, [CHRG](#chrg) charge, [WDL](#wdl) partial withdrawal, [ASHR](#ashr) asset share, [MVR](#mvr) market value reduction

### UNIT

- **Full term:** Unit
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** unit-linked
- **Definition:** As an item, one unit of an investment fund held for a policy. As a kind, the part of a reserve or cash flow that relates to the unit fund.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not NUNIT. Not a unit of measure. Not CU (coverage units).
- **Related terms:** [NUNIT](#nunit) non-unit, [UPRC](#uprc) unit price, [FV](#fv) fund value, [ALLOC](#alloc) allocation, [RES](#res) reserve, [BID](#bid) bid, [OFFER](#offer) offer

### NUNIT

- **Full term:** Non-unit
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** sterling, rand or dollar reserve
- **Definition:** Narrows a reserve or cash flow to the part held outside the unit fund, covering expenses and benefits that charges do not meet.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not UNIT.
- **Related terms:** [UNIT](#unit) unit, [RES](#res) reserve, [CHRG](#chrg) charge, [FV](#fv) fund value

### UPRC

- **Full term:** Unit price
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The price of one unit of an investment fund at the stated time point.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PRC (pricing basis). Not UPR (unearned premium reserve).
- **Related terms:** [UNIT](#unit) unit, [FV](#fv) fund value, [INVR](#invr) investment return, [BID](#bid) bid, [OFFER](#offer) offer

### ALLOC

- **Full term:** Allocation
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** invested premium
- **Definition:** Narrows a premium to the part that is credited to the policyholder's fund.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not the whole premium. Not an allocation of expenses to products, which this version does not cover.
- **Related terms:** [PREM](#prem) premium, [FV](#fv) fund value, [UNIT](#unit) unit, [CHRG](#chrg) charge, [OFFER](#offer) offer

### CHRG

- **Full term:** Charge
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** fee, deduction
- **Definition:** An amount the insurer deducts from the policyholder's fund or premium under the policy terms.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CHG (change in a balance). Not EXP: a charge is income to the insurer and an expense is a cost. Not PREM.
- **Related terms:** [FMC](#fmc) fund management charge, [RISK](#risk) risk premium, [FV](#fv) fund value, [ALLOC](#alloc) allocation, [PREM](#prem) premium, [NUNIT](#nunit) non-unit, [SV](#sv) surrender value, [CHG](#chg) change, [POLF](#polf) policy fee

### FMC

- **Full term:** Fund management charge
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** annual management charge, asset-based fee
- **Definition:** Narrows a charge to one taken as a proportion of the fund value.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not the investment expense the insurer itself pays, which is EXP with kind INV.
- **Related terms:** [CHRG](#chrg) charge, [FV](#fv) fund value, [INV](#inv) investment, [POLF](#polf) policy fee

### BON

- **Full term:** Bonus
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** profit share, declared bonus
- **Definition:** An addition to benefits on with-profits business, made at the insurer's discretion out of surplus.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ESC, which is contractual. Not a commission bonus to an intermediary.
- **Related terms:** [RVS](#rvs) reversionary, [TMNL](#tmnl) terminal, [ASHR](#ashr) asset share, [PH](#ph) policyholder, [BEN](#ben) benefit, [SA](#sa) sum assured, [GTEE](#gtee) guarantee, [ESC](#esc) escalation, [MVR](#mvr) market value reduction, [WP](#wp) with-profits, [UNDL](#undl) underlying items, [LAC](#lac) loss-absorbing capacity, [BSR](#bsr) bonus stabilisation reserve

### RVS

- **Full term:** Reversionary
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** regular bonus, annual bonus, vested bonus
- **Definition:** Narrows a bonus to one that is added during the term and is guaranteed once declared.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not TMNL. Not REV as a short form of revenue, for which see IREV.
- **Related terms:** [TMNL](#tmnl) terminal, [BON](#bon) bonus, [GTEE](#gtee) guarantee

### TMNL

- **Full term:** Terminal
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** final bonus, non-vested bonus
- **Definition:** Narrows a bonus to one that is added only when a claim is paid and is not guaranteed before then.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not TERM (policy term). Not RVS.
- **Related terms:** [RVS](#rvs) reversionary, [BON](#bon) bonus, [ASHR](#ashr) asset share

### ASHR

- **Full term:** Asset share
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The accumulation of premiums less expenses, charges and the cost of cover, with the investment return actually earned, for a with-profits policy.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FV, which is a contractual balance. Asset share is a calculated guide to a fair payout. In South African guidance on smoothed bonus business the term can mean the fund account plus its share of the bonus stabilisation reserve.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 110 version 5, definitions
- **Related terms:** [FV](#fv) fund value, [BON](#bon) bonus, [TMNL](#tmnl) terminal, [SV](#sv) surrender value, [PH](#ph) policyholder, [MVR](#mvr) market value reduction, [WP](#wp) with-profits

### GTEE

- **Full term:** Guarantee
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** guaranteed benefit, embedded investment derivative
- **Definition:** A benefit level the insurer must pay whatever the investment outcome. As a kind it narrows a benefit to the guaranteed part.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not TVOG, which is the cost of the guarantee and not its level.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 110 version 5, definitions
- **Related terms:** [TVOG](#tvog) time value of options and guarantees, [BEN](#ben) benefit, [BON](#bon) bonus, [RVS](#rvs) reversionary, [MAT](#mat) maturity, [RN](#rn) risk neutral, [ANTY](#anty) annuity, [NPAR](#npar) without-profits

### BID

- **Full term:** Bid
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** bid price, cancellation price
- **Definition:** Narrows a unit price to the price at which the insurer cancels units when benefits are paid.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not OFFER. The expropriation price, which is the amount taken out of the fund when a unit is cancelled, is a different thing and has no token yet.
- **Related terms:** [OFFER](#offer) offer, [UPRC](#uprc) unit price, [UNIT](#unit) unit, [FV](#fv) fund value

### OFFER

- **Full term:** Offer
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** offer price, creation price for the policyholder
- **Definition:** Narrows a unit price to the price at which the insurer allocates units to a policy.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not BID. The appropriation price, which is the amount put into the fund when a unit is created, is a different thing and has no token yet.
- **Related terms:** [BID](#bid) bid, [UPRC](#uprc) unit price, [UNIT](#unit) unit, [ALLOC](#alloc) allocation

### MVR

- **Full term:** Market value reduction
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** market value adjustment
- **Definition:** The amount by which a payment on surrender or withdrawal from a with-profits fund is reduced when the assets are worth less than the declared value.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a surrender charge, which is CHRG with kind SUR and is set in advance.
- **Related terms:** [SV](#sv) surrender value, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [WDL](#wdl) partial withdrawal, [ASHR](#ashr) asset share, [WP](#wp) with-profits, [BON](#bon) bonus

### POLF

- **Full term:** Policy fee
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** per policy charge, flat fee
- **Definition:** Narrows a charge or premium to a fixed amount per policy that does not depend on the size of the benefit.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FMC, which varies with the fund.
- **Related terms:** [CHRG](#chrg) charge, [FMC](#fmc) fund management charge, [PREM](#prem) premium, [OH](#oh) overhead, [MAINT](#maint) maintenance

### WP

- **Full term:** With-profits
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** participating
- **Definition:** Narrows an item to business where the policyholder is entitled to a share of surplus, usually at the insurer's discretion.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not NPAR. Not unit-linked business, which is UNIT.
- **Related terms:** [NPAR](#npar) without-profits, [BON](#bon) bonus, [ASHR](#ashr) asset share, [MVR](#mvr) market value reduction, [PH](#ph) policyholder

### NPAR

- **Full term:** Without-profits
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** non-participating, non-profit
- **Definition:** Narrows an item to business where the benefits, or the way they are calculated, are fixed at the start and the insurer has no discretion over them.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not WP. Unit-linked business is usually treated as its own class.
- **Related terms:** [WP](#wp) with-profits, [GTEE](#gtee) guarantee

## Economic and investment

Interest, discounting, investment return and assets.

### DISC

- **Full term:** Discount
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** discounting, valuation interest
- **Definition:** The interest allowance used to turn future amounts into present values. It takes RATE, FORCE or FAC as its measure.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DIS (disability). Not DCON (discontinuance). Not a premium discount. Not INT or INVR, which describe what is earned or credited.
- **Related terms:** [INT](#int) interest, [INVR](#invr) investment return, [FWD](#fwd) forward, [SPOT](#spot) spot, [PV](#pv) present value, [FAC](#fac) factor, [FORCE](#force) force, [IFL](#ifl) IFRS 17 locked-in, [IFC](#ifc) IFRS 17 current, [RN](#rn) risk neutral, [INFL](#infl) inflation, [UNW](#unw) unwind of discount, [IRR](#irr) internal rate of return

### INT

- **Full term:** Interest
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** credited interest
- **Definition:** Interest earned on or credited to a stated balance in the model period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DISC, which is used for valuing. Not INVR, which includes capital gains. Not INIT.
- **Related terms:** [DISC](#disc) discount, [INVR](#invr) investment return, [INVI](#invi) investment income, [ACC](#acc) accumulated, [ACCR](#accr) interest accretion, [FWD](#fwd) forward, [SPOT](#spot) spot

### FWD

- **Full term:** Forward
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** forward rate
- **Definition:** Narrows an interest or discount rate to the rate that applies over one future period, from one future date to the next.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SPOT. Not carried forward.
- **Related terms:** [SPOT](#spot) spot, [DISC](#disc) discount, [INT](#int) interest

### SPOT

- **Full term:** Spot
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** zero coupon rate
- **Definition:** Narrows an interest or discount rate to the average rate that applies from the valuation date to one future date.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FWD.
- **Related terms:** [FWD](#fwd) forward, [DISC](#disc) discount, [INT](#int) interest

### INVI

- **Full term:** Investment income
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** interest, dividends and rent
- **Definition:** Income received from assets in the model period as interest, dividends and rent. It excludes changes in asset values.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INVR, which includes gains. Not INCM (income benefit). Not INVC.
- **Related terms:** [INVR](#invr) investment return, [INT](#int) interest, [INV](#inv) investment, [ASSET](#asset) assets, [TAX](#tax) tax

### INVR

- **Full term:** Investment return
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** total return
- **Definition:** Investment income plus realised and unrealised changes in asset values in the model period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INVI. Not INT. Not DISC.
- **Related terms:** [INVI](#invi) investment income, [INT](#int) interest, [INV](#inv) investment, [ASSET](#asset) assets, [DISC](#disc) discount, [RW](#rw) real world, [UPRC](#uprc) unit price, [EQ](#eq) equity

### ASSET

- **Full term:** Assets
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** investments
- **Definition:** The assets held to back liabilities or capital, valued at the stated time point.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ASHR (asset share). Not FV.
- **Related terms:** [INVI](#invi) investment income, [INVR](#invr) investment return, [INV](#inv) investment, [EQ](#eq) equity, [RES](#res) reserve, [OF](#of) own funds, [UNDL](#undl) underlying items, [PROP](#prop) property, [SURP](#surp) surplus

### EQ

- **Full term:** Equity
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** shares, ordinary shares
- **Definition:** Narrows assets, returns or capital to listed or unlisted shares.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not shareholders' equity on the balance sheet, which this version does not name. Not equalisation.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(5)(b)
- **Related terms:** [ASSET](#asset) assets, [INVR](#invr) investment return, [MKT](#mkt) market risk, [PROP](#prop) property

## Reinsurance

Tokens specific to reinsurance. The parties RI and NET are in the Party family.

### RET

- **Full term:** Retention
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** retained amount, retention limit
- **Definition:** The amount of risk on one life or one policy that the insurer keeps for its own account before reinsurance applies.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RETR (retrenchment). Not retained earnings. Not PERS, which is retention of customers.
- **Related terms:** [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded, [NET](#net) net of reinsurance, [QS](#qs) quota share, [XL](#xl) excess of loss, [SA](#sa) sum assured, [SAR](#sar) sum at risk, [FACL](#facl) facultative, [RETR](#retr) retrenchment

### QS

- **Full term:** Quota share
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** proportional reinsurance
- **Definition:** Narrows a reinsurance item to a treaty where a fixed proportion of every risk is ceded.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not XL. Not surplus reinsurance, where the proportion varies by size of risk and which this version does not name.
- **Related terms:** [XL](#xl) excess of loss, [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded, [RET](#ret) retention, [SA](#sa) sum assured, [TRTY](#trty) treaty, [COINS](#coins) coinsurance

### XL

- **Full term:** Excess of loss
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** non-proportional reinsurance
- **Definition:** Narrows a reinsurance item to a treaty where the reinsurer pays the part of a loss above a stated limit.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not QS.
- **Related terms:** [QS](#qs) quota share, [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded, [RET](#ret) retention, [CAT](#cat) catastrophe risk, [TRTY](#trty) treaty

### FACL

- **Full term:** Facultative
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** case by case reinsurance
- **Definition:** Narrows a reinsurance item to cover arranged risk by risk, with terms agreed for each one.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FAC (factor). Not TRTY.
- **Related terms:** [TRTY](#trty) treaty, [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded, [RET](#ret) retention

### TRTY

- **Full term:** Treaty
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** automatic reinsurance, obligatory reinsurance
- **Definition:** Narrows a reinsurance item to cover under a standing agreement, where every policy within its scope is reinsured on the agreed terms.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FACL.
- **Related terms:** [FACL](#facl) facultative, [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded, [QS](#qs) quota share, [XL](#xl) excess of loss, [COINS](#coins) coinsurance

### COINS

- **Full term:** Coinsurance
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** original terms reinsurance
- **Definition:** Narrows a reinsurance item to a treaty where the reinsurer shares in every part of the original contract, including premiums, claims, reserves and expenses.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not risk premium reinsurance, where only the sum at risk is reinsured on the reinsurer's own rates, and which uses RISK. Not co-insurance between two direct insurers.
- **Related terms:** [TRTY](#trty) treaty, [QS](#qs) quota share, [RISK](#risk) risk premium, [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded

## Reserves and liabilities

Amounts held for future obligations, outside IFRS 17 specific terms.

### RES

- **Full term:** Reserve
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** provision, policy liability, mathematical reserve
- **Definition:** The amount held at the stated time point to meet future obligations on the stated basis. It is the generic term and the description must give the basis.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not BEL, TP or LRC, which are reserves defined by a specific framework. Not REC or REM.
- **Related terms:** [BEL](#bel) best estimate liability, [TP](#tp) technical provisions, [UPR](#upr) unearned premium reserve, [IBNR](#ibnr) incurred but not reported, [OCR](#ocr) outstanding claims reserve, [UNIT](#unit) unit, [NUNIT](#nunit) non-unit, [SAR](#sar) sum at risk, [CHG](#chg) change, [ASSET](#asset) assets, [LRC](#lrc) liability for remaining coverage, [LIC](#lic) liability for incurred claims, [NETP](#netp) net premium, [SURP](#surp) surplus

### BEL

- **Full term:** Best estimate liability
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** best estimate of liabilities
- **Definition:** The probability-weighted present value of future cash outflows less inflows on best estimate assumptions, with no margins. Under Solvency II and the South African standards it is discounted at the prescribed risk-free rates.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not BE, which is the basis. Not TP, which adds the risk margin. Not FCF, which adds the IFRS 17 risk adjustment.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 77(2). South African Prudential Standard FSI 2.2 section 6
- **Related terms:** [BE](#be) best estimate, [TP](#tp) technical provisions, [RM](#rm) risk margin, [RES](#res) reserve, [FCF](#fcf) fulfilment cash flows, [PV](#pv) present value, [UNW](#unw) unwind of discount, [TVOG](#tvog) time value of options and guarantees

### TP

- **Full term:** Technical provisions
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The amount the insurer would have to pay to transfer its insurance obligations to another insurer today. It is the best estimate liability plus the risk margin.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not BEL alone. Not total and permanent disability.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Articles 76 and 77. South African Prudential Standard FSI 2.2
- **Related terms:** [BEL](#bel) best estimate liability, [RM](#rm) risk margin, [OF](#of) own funds, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [SII](#sii) Solvency II, [SAM](#sam) Solvency Assessment and Management, [RES](#res) reserve

### RM

- **Full term:** Risk margin
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** cost of capital margin
- **Definition:** The amount added to the best estimate liability under a Solvency II style regime. It is the cost of holding own funds equal to the SCR for as long as the obligations take to run off.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RA, which is the IFRS 17 measure and can use other methods. Not MGN. The cost of capital rate is 6% a year under Solvency II and in South Africa. The Solvency II rate changes to 4.75% from 30 January 2027.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 77(3) and 77(5), Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Articles 37 to 39, Directive (EU) 2025/2. South African Prudential Standard FSI 2.2 section 14
- **Related terms:** [RA](#ra) risk adjustment, [TP](#tp) technical provisions, [BEL](#bel) best estimate liability, [COC](#coc) cost of capital, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [MGN](#mgn) margin, [SII](#sii) Solvency II, [SAM](#sam) Solvency Assessment and Management

### UPR

- **Full term:** Unearned premium reserve
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** unearned premium provision
- **Definition:** The part of premiums already received that relates to cover in periods after the stated time point.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not UPRC (unit price).
- **Related terms:** [RES](#res) reserve, [IBNR](#ibnr) incurred but not reported, [OCR](#ocr) outstanding claims reserve, [PREM](#prem) premium, [PAA](#paa) premium allocation approach

### IBNR

- **Full term:** Incurred but not reported
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The reserve for claims, and the related expenses, where the event has happened but the insurer has not yet been told.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not OCR, which covers claims already reported.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 116 version 1 section 3
- **Related terms:** [OCR](#ocr) outstanding claims reserve, [RES](#res) reserve, [CLM](#clm) claim, [LIC](#lic) liability for incurred claims, [UPR](#upr) unearned premium reserve

### OCR

- **Full term:** Outstanding claims reserve
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** notified claims reserve, case reserve
- **Definition:** The reserve for claims that have been reported and not yet paid in full.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not IBNR. Not OCI. Some guidance uses outstanding claims provision as a wider heading that includes IBNR. In this standard it is reported claims only.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 116 version 1 section 2
- **Related terms:** [IBNR](#ibnr) incurred but not reported, [RES](#res) reserve, [CLM](#clm) claim, [LIC](#lic) liability for incurred claims, [UPR](#upr) unearned premium reserve

## IFRS 17

Terms defined by IFRS 17 Insurance Contracts.

### CSM

- **Full term:** Contractual service margin
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** unearned profit
- **Definition:** The part of the carrying amount of a group of insurance contracts that holds unearned profit, recognised as insurance contract services are provided. For reinsurance held it is a net cost or net gain and can take either sign.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not VIF or PROF. Not RA. It cannot be negative for contracts issued: any excess is a loss and is tracked in LC.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A and paragraphs 38, 43, 44, 48 and 65
- **Related terms:** [RA](#ra) risk adjustment, [LC](#lc) loss component, [FCF](#fcf) fulfilment cash flows, [LRC](#lrc) liability for remaining coverage, [CU](#cu) coverage units, [ACCR](#accr) interest accretion, [REL](#rel) release, [ESTCH](#estch) change in estimates, [IFL](#ifl) IFRS 17 locked-in, [ONER](#oner) onerous, [NB](#nb) new business, [GMM](#gmm) general measurement model, [VFA](#vfa) variable fee approach

### RA

- **Full term:** Risk adjustment
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** risk adjustment for non-financial risk
- **Definition:** The compensation the insurer requires under IFRS 17 for bearing uncertainty in the amount and timing of cash flows from non-financial risk.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RM, which is the Solvency II style measure. Not MGN. Not RI.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A and paragraph 37
- **Related terms:** [RM](#rm) risk margin, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [FCF](#fcf) fulfilment cash flows, [MGN](#mgn) margin, [LRC](#lrc) liability for remaining coverage, [LIC](#lic) liability for incurred claims, [REL](#rel) release

### LC

- **Full term:** Loss component
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** onerous contract loss
- **Definition:** The tracked part of the liability for remaining coverage that holds losses recognised on an onerous group. Its run-off is shown as a reversal of losses and kept out of revenue, and it ends at zero.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LIC or LRC. Not a negative CSM, which does not exist.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 49 to 52
- **Related terms:** [ONER](#oner) onerous, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [LRC](#lrc) liability for remaining coverage, [LOREC](#lorec) loss-recovery component, [ISE](#ise) insurance service expenses

### LOREC

- **Full term:** Loss-recovery component
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The part of the asset for remaining coverage on reinsurance held that records recoveries of losses on onerous underlying contracts. It cannot exceed the share of the underlying loss component that is expected to be recovered.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LC, which sits on the underlying contracts.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 66A and 66B
- **Related terms:** [LC](#lc) loss component, [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded, [ONER](#oner) onerous

### LRC

- **Full term:** Liability for remaining coverage
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The insurer's obligation for insured events that have not yet happened and for services not yet provided, measured under IFRS 17. It is the fulfilment cash flows for future service plus the CSM.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LIC. Not LC, which is one part of it.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A and paragraph 40(a)
- **Related terms:** [LIC](#lic) liability for incurred claims, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [FCF](#fcf) fulfilment cash flows, [RA](#ra) risk adjustment, [LC](#lc) loss component, [RES](#res) reserve, [IREV](#irev) insurance revenue, [PAA](#paa) premium allocation approach

### LIC

- **Full term:** Liability for incurred claims
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The insurer's obligation to investigate and pay claims for insured events that have already happened, whether reported or not, plus other incurred insurance expenses, measured under IFRS 17.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LRC. Not LC.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A and paragraph 40(b)
- **Related terms:** [LRC](#lrc) liability for remaining coverage, [IBNR](#ibnr) incurred but not reported, [OCR](#ocr) outstanding claims reserve, [RES](#res) reserve, [RA](#ra) risk adjustment, [ISE](#ise) insurance service expenses

### FCF

- **Full term:** Fulfilment cash flows
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The unbiased, probability-weighted present value of future cash outflows less inflows within the contract boundary, plus the risk adjustment, under IFRS 17.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CF or NCF, which are undiscounted flows in a period. Not BEL, which has no risk adjustment.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A and paragraph 32
- **Related terms:** [BEL](#bel) best estimate liability, [RA](#ra) risk adjustment, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [LRC](#lrc) liability for remaining coverage, [CF](#cf) cash flow, [ATTR](#attr) directly attributable, [NATTR](#nattr) not directly attributable, [IFC](#ifc) IFRS 17 current, [GMM](#gmm) general measurement model

### CU

- **Full term:** Coverage units
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The quantity of insurance contract services in a group of contracts, based on the benefits provided and the expected coverage period. The CSM is released in proportion to the units provided in the period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CUM. Not UNIT. Not CI.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraph B119
- **Related terms:** [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [REL](#rel) release, [FUT](#fut) future total

### IACF

- **Full term:** Insurance acquisition cash flows
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** Cash flows from selling, underwriting and starting a group of insurance contracts that are directly attributable to the portfolio the group belongs to, even if not to a single contract. Initial commission usually qualifies.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not EXP with kind ACQ, which excludes commission and may include costs that are not attributable. Recurring commission is a maintenance cost under IFRS 17 and not an acquisition cash flow.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A and paragraphs 28A, B35A and B65
- **Related terms:** [ACQ](#acq) acquisition, [COMM](#comm) commission, [EXP](#exp) expense, [IREV](#irev) insurance revenue, [ISE](#ise) insurance service expenses

### IFE

- **Full term:** Insurance finance income or expenses
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** insurance finance expense
- **Definition:** The change in the carrying amount of a group of insurance contracts that comes from the effect of, and changes in, the time value of money and financial risk.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ISE. Not IFL or IFC. Not INVR, which is the return on assets.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraph 87
- **Related terms:** [OCI](#oci) other comprehensive income, [PL](#pl) profit or loss, [ACCR](#accr) interest accretion, [UNW](#unw) unwind of discount, [IFL](#ifl) IFRS 17 locked-in, [IFC](#ifc) IFRS 17 current, [ISR](#isr) insurance service result

### IREV

- **Full term:** Insurance revenue
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The amount recognised under IFRS 17 for services provided in the period. It excludes investment components.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PREM. Revenue is earned and premium is received. Not RVS.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 83 and 85 and B120 to B124
- **Related terms:** [ISE](#ise) insurance service expenses, [ISR](#isr) insurance service result, [INVC](#invc) investment component, [LRC](#lrc) liability for remaining coverage, [IACF](#iacf) insurance acquisition cash flows, [REL](#rel) release

### ISE

- **Full term:** Insurance service expenses
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** Incurred claims without investment components, other incurred insurance service expenses, amortisation of acquisition cash flows, changes in the liability for incurred claims, and losses on onerous groups and their reversals, under IFRS 17.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not IFE. Not EXP.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 84 and 103
- **Related terms:** [IREV](#irev) insurance revenue, [ISR](#isr) insurance service result, [IACF](#iacf) insurance acquisition cash flows, [LC](#lc) loss component, [LIC](#lic) liability for incurred claims, [ATTR](#attr) directly attributable, [EXP](#exp) expense

### ISR

- **Full term:** Insurance service result
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** Insurance revenue less insurance service expenses.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PROF, which also includes investment and finance results. Income and expenses from reinsurance held are presented separately from it.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 80, 82 and 86
- **Related terms:** [IREV](#irev) insurance revenue, [ISE](#ise) insurance service expenses, [IFE](#ife) insurance finance income or expenses, [PROF](#prof) profit

### OCI

- **Full term:** Other comprehensive income
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Definition:** The part of comprehensive income that sits outside profit or loss. Under IFRS 17 it takes the part of insurance finance income or expenses that is not allocated to profit or loss, when that option is chosen. As a kind it narrows an item to the part presented there.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not OCR.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 88 to 90
- **Related terms:** [PL](#pl) profit or loss, [IFE](#ife) insurance finance income or expenses, [IFL](#ifl) IFRS 17 locked-in, [IFC](#ifc) IFRS 17 current

### PL

- **Full term:** Profit or loss
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** income statement, P&L
- **Definition:** Narrows an item to the part presented in the statement of profit or loss.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PROF, which is the profit amount itself. Not POL.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraph 80
- **Related terms:** [OCI](#oci) other comprehensive income, [IFE](#ife) insurance finance income or expenses, [PROF](#prof) profit

### INVC

- **Full term:** Investment component
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Definition:** The amount an insurance contract requires the insurer to repay to the policyholder in all circumstances, whether or not an insured event happens.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not INV, INVI or INVR. Not FV: the investment component is often but not always the fund value. The definition covers both distinct and non-distinct components.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A and paragraphs 11 and 85
- **Related terms:** [IREV](#irev) insurance revenue, [CLM](#clm) claim, [INV](#inv) investment

### ONER

- **Full term:** Onerous
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** loss-making
- **Definition:** The state of a contract or group that is expected to make a loss. A contract is onerous at inception if its fulfilment cash flows, earlier acquisition cash flows and cash flows on that date are a net outflow. A group becomes onerous later when unfavourable changes exceed the CSM.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a statement about a single policy unless the description says so.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 47 and 48
- **Related terms:** [LC](#lc) loss component, [LOREC](#lorec) loss-recovery component, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [IND](#ind) indicator

### GMM

- **Full term:** General measurement model
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** general model, building block approach
- **Definition:** The default IFRS 17 measurement approach: fulfilment cash flows plus a CSM. As an item it takes IND or FLG to mark a group measured this way.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PAA or VFA. The name is an industry label and is not used in the standard itself.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 32 to 52
- **Related terms:** [PAA](#paa) premium allocation approach, [VFA](#vfa) variable fee approach, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [FCF](#fcf) fulfilment cash flows

### PAA

- **Full term:** Premium allocation approach
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** simplified approach
- **Definition:** The simplified IFRS 17 approach for the liability for remaining coverage, allowed for short contracts and where it gives a close result. As an item it takes IND or FLG to mark a group measured this way.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not GMM or VFA. It applies to remaining coverage only. Incurred claims are still measured with fulfilment cash flows.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 53 to 59
- **Related terms:** [GMM](#gmm) general measurement model, [VFA](#vfa) variable fee approach, [LRC](#lrc) liability for remaining coverage, [UPR](#upr) unearned premium reserve

### VFA

- **Full term:** Variable fee approach
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** direct participating contracts
- **Definition:** The IFRS 17 approach for contracts with direct participation features, where the insurer's share of the underlying items is treated as a fee. As an item it takes IND or FLG to mark a group measured this way.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not GMM or PAA. The name is an industry label. The standard speaks of insurance contracts with direct participation features.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A and paragraphs 45 and B101
- **Related terms:** [GMM](#gmm) general measurement model, [PAA](#paa) premium allocation approach, [UNDL](#undl) underlying items, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin

### UNDL

- **Full term:** Underlying items
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The items that determine some of the amounts payable to policyholders on participating contracts, such as a pool of assets.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not UNIT. Not ASSET in general: only the items the contract refers to.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A
- **Related terms:** [VFA](#vfa) variable fee approach, [ASSET](#asset) assets, [FV](#fv) fund value, [BON](#bon) bonus

## Movements

Tokens for the MOVE slot. They turn a balance into the flow that explains part of its change.

### NB

- **Full term:** New business
- **Slot:** MOVE
- **Also called:** new contracts, sales
- **Definition:** The part of a flow or of the change in a balance that comes from contracts first recognised in the model period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not VNB, which is a value measure. Not RINST. Not NTU.
- **Related terms:** [VNB](#vnb) value of new business, [APE](#ape) annual premium equivalent, [ACQ](#acq) acquisition, [INC](#inc) inception, [IF](#if) in force, [POL](#pol) policy, [RINST](#rinst) reinstatement, [NTU](#ntu) not taken up, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [CHG](#chg) change, [NBS](#nbs) new business strain

### CHG

- **Full term:** Change
- **Slot:** MOVE
- **Also called:** movement, increase
- **Definition:** The closing balance less the opening balance over the model period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CHRG (a charge). Not ESTCH, which is one cause of change.
- **Related terms:** [CHRG](#chrg) charge, [ESTCH](#estch) change in estimates, [EXPV](#expv) experience variance, [UNW](#unw) unwind of discount, [REL](#rel) release, [NB](#nb) new business, [RES](#res) reserve

### ACCR

- **Full term:** Interest accretion
- **Slot:** MOVE
- **Also called:** interest accreted
- **Definition:** The interest added to the CSM in the period. Under the general model it uses the discount rates set at the initial recognition of the group.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not UNW, which applies to present values of cash flows. Not ACC or ACCD.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 44(b) and B72(b)
- **Related terms:** [UNW](#unw) unwind of discount, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [IFL](#ifl) IFRS 17 locked-in, [IFE](#ife) insurance finance income or expenses, [INT](#int) interest, [ACC](#acc) accumulated

### UNW

- **Full term:** Unwind of discount
- **Slot:** MOVE
- **Also called:** interest on the liability, effect of the passage of time
- **Definition:** The increase in a present value over the period that comes only from being one period closer to the cash flows.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ACCR, which applies to the CSM. Not INVR. IFRS 17 does not use the word unwind: the effect is part of insurance finance income or expenses.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraph 87
- **Related terms:** [ACCR](#accr) interest accretion, [DISC](#disc) discount, [BEL](#bel) best estimate liability, [IFE](#ife) insurance finance income or expenses, [CHG](#chg) change

### REL

- **Full term:** Release
- **Slot:** MOVE
- **Also called:** amortisation, run-off, recognition in profit
- **Definition:** The part of a balance taken to profit in the period because service was provided or risk expired.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not REM (remaining). Not REC. Not a reversal of a loss.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 44(e), B119 and B124
- **Related terms:** [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin, [RA](#ra) risk adjustment, [CU](#cu) coverage units, [IREV](#irev) insurance revenue, [CHG](#chg) change

### ESTCH

- **Full term:** Change in estimates
- **Slot:** MOVE
- **Also called:** assumption change, basis change
- **Definition:** The change in a balance caused by revising the estimates of future cash flows or the assumptions behind them. Under IFRS 17, changes that relate to future service adjust the CSM, and changes in financial assumptions go to insurance finance income or expenses.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not EXPV, which compares actual with expected for the current period. Not CHG, which is the whole change.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 paragraphs 44(c), B96 and B97
- **Related terms:** [EXPV](#expv) experience variance, [CHG](#chg) change, [CSM](#csm) contractual service margin

### EXPV

- **Full term:** Experience variance
- **Slot:** MOVE
- **Also called:** experience adjustment, actual versus expected
- **Definition:** The difference between what was expected for the period at its start and what actually happened. Under IFRS 17 the term covers premiums and related cash flows, and insurance service expenses other than acquisition expenses.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not ESTCH, which concerns future periods. Not EXP or EXPY. Not ADJ.
- **Source checked:** IFRS 17 Appendix A and paragraph B96(a)
- **Related terms:** [ESTCH](#estch) change in estimates, [CHG](#chg) change, [ADJ](#adj) adjustment

## Capital and risk

Capital amounts and the risks they are held for.

### CAP

- **Full term:** Capital
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** Assets held in excess of liabilities, in general. The description must say which measure of capital it means.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RCAP, SCR or OF, which are specific measures. Not a capital (lump sum) benefit, which is LS.
- **Related terms:** [RCAP](#rcap) required capital, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [MCR](#mcr) minimum capital requirement, [OF](#of) own funds, [FSUR](#fsur) free surplus, [COC](#coc) cost of capital, [CAR](#car) capital adequacy requirement

### RCAP

- **Full term:** Required capital
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** capital requirement, locked-in capital
- **Definition:** Assets held above liabilities whose distribution to shareholders is restricted, on the stated basis. It reflects the company's own targets and is at least the regulatory requirement.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SCR, which is one regulatory definition of it. Not FSUR.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 107 version 8 sections 2.5 and 3.5
- **Related terms:** [CAP](#cap) capital, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [MCR](#mcr) minimum capital requirement, [FSUR](#fsur) free surplus, [COC](#coc) cost of capital, [EV](#ev) embedded value, [OF](#of) own funds, [DISTE](#diste) distributable earnings, [ANW](#anw) adjusted net worth, [NBS](#nbs) new business strain

### SCR

- **Full term:** Solvency capital requirement
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The capital needed so that the insurer's own funds survive the losses of one year with 99.5% confidence, as defined under Solvency II and under the South African standards.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not MCR. Not SAR. Not RCAP, which may be set higher by the company.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 101. South African Prudential Standard FSI 1 section 6.1
- **Related terms:** [MCR](#mcr) minimum capital requirement, [OF](#of) own funds, [RCAP](#rcap) required capital, [CAP](#cap) capital, [TP](#tp) technical provisions, [RM](#rm) risk margin, [MORT](#mort) mortality risk, [LONG](#long) longevity risk, [MORB](#morb) morbidity risk, [CAT](#cat) catastrophe risk, [MKT](#mkt) market risk, [SPRD](#sprd) spread risk, [FX](#fx) currency risk, [CPTY](#cpty) counterparty default risk, [OPR](#opr) operational risk, [DIV](#div) diversification, [SHK](#shk) shock, [SII](#sii) Solvency II, [SAM](#sam) Solvency Assessment and Management, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk, [REVN](#revn) revision risk, [CONC](#conc) concentration risk, [LAC](#lac) loss-absorbing capacity, [CAR](#car) capital adequacy requirement

### MCR

- **Full term:** Minimum capital requirement
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The lowest regulatory capital level. Under Solvency II and in South Africa it is calibrated to 85% confidence over one year and kept between 25% and 45% of the SCR, with an absolute floor in money terms.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SCR.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 129. South African Prudential Standard FSI 1 section 6.2 and FSI 3
- **Related terms:** [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [OF](#of) own funds, [CAP](#cap) capital, [RCAP](#rcap) required capital, [SII](#sii) Solvency II, [SAM](#sam) Solvency Assessment and Management, [CAR](#car) capital adequacy requirement

### OF

- **Full term:** Own funds
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** eligible capital, available capital
- **Definition:** The regulatory capital that counts towards the capital requirements. It is basic own funds plus approved ancillary own funds, after the limits that apply to each tier.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not OFF (office premium). Not FSUR.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Articles 87 to 98. South African Prudential Standard FSI 1 section 5
- **Related terms:** [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [MCR](#mcr) minimum capital requirement, [CAP](#cap) capital, [TP](#tp) technical provisions, [ASSET](#asset) assets, [FSUR](#fsur) free surplus, [RCAP](#rcap) required capital, [SII](#sii) Solvency II, [SURP](#surp) surplus

### MORT

- **Full term:** Mortality risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk that death rates are higher than expected, in level, trend or volatility.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DTH, which is the event of death. Not LONG, which is the opposite risk. The standard formula shock is a permanent 15% rise in death rates under both Solvency II and the South African standards.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(3)(a), Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 137. South African Prudential Standard FSI 4.2 section 5
- **Related terms:** [LONG](#long) longevity risk, [MORB](#morb) morbidity risk, [CAT](#cat) catastrophe risk, [DTH](#dth) death, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [SHK](#shk) shock

### LONG

- **Full term:** Longevity risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk that death rates are lower than expected, so that annuities are paid for longer.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not MORT. Not a long position or a long term. The Solvency II shock is a permanent 20% fall in death rates. The South African shock is a 10% fall plus an extra 1% a year of improvement.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(3)(b), Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 138. South African Prudential Standard FSI 4.2 section 6
- **Related terms:** [MORT](#mort) mortality risk, [DTH](#dth) death, [ANTY](#anty) annuity, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [IMP](#imp) improvement, [REVN](#revn) revision risk

### MORB

- **Full term:** Morbidity risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** disability-morbidity risk, disability risk
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk that disability or sickness claims are worse than expected, including recoveries that are slower than expected.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DIS or CI, which are the events.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(3)(c), Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 139. South African Prudential Standard FSI 4.2 section 7
- **Related terms:** [MORT](#mort) mortality risk, [DIS](#dis) disability, [CI](#ci) critical illness, [REC](#rec) recovery, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [REVN](#revn) revision risk, [HIV](#hiv) HIV and AIDS

### CAT

- **Full term:** Catastrophe risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk of loss from extreme or irregular events, such as a pandemic, that cause many claims in a short time.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a category.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(3)(g), Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 143. South African Prudential Standard FSI 4.2 section 10
- **Related terms:** [MORT](#mort) mortality risk, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [SHK](#shk) shock, [XL](#xl) excess of loss, [MASS](#mass) mass

### MKT

- **Full term:** Market risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk of loss from changes in market prices and rates.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not marketing.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Articles 13(31) and 105(5)
- **Related terms:** [EQ](#eq) equity, [SPRD](#sprd) spread risk, [FX](#fx) currency risk, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [PROP](#prop) property, [CONC](#conc) concentration risk

### SPRD

- **Full term:** Spread risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** credit spread risk
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk of loss from changes in credit spreads over the risk-free rate.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CPTY, which is loss from a counterparty failing to pay.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(5)(d)
- **Related terms:** [MKT](#mkt) market risk, [CPTY](#cpty) counterparty default risk, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement

### FX

- **Full term:** Currency risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** foreign exchange risk
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk of loss from changes in exchange rates.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not an exchange rate itself.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(5)(e)
- **Related terms:** [MKT](#mkt) market risk, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement

### CPTY

- **Full term:** Counterparty default risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** credit default risk
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk that a reinsurer, bank or other counterparty defaults or that its credit standing worsens, for exposures not covered by spread risk.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SPRD.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(6)
- **Related terms:** [SPRD](#sprd) spread risk, [RI](#ri) reinsurance ceded, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [CONC](#conc) concentration risk

### OPR

- **Full term:** Operational risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount to the risk of loss from failed processes, people, systems or external events. It includes legal risk and excludes strategic and reputational risk.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not operating profit.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Articles 13(33) and 101(4)
- **Related terms:** [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement

### DIV

- **Full term:** Diversification
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** diversification benefit
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount to the reduction that arises because the separate risks are not expected to happen together.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a dividend. Not DIS.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 13(37)
- **Related terms:** [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [LAC](#lac) loss-absorbing capacity

### UP

- **Full term:** Up
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** upward shock, increase
- **Definition:** Narrows a shock or capital amount to the case where the assumption rises.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not UPR or UPRC.
- **Source checked:** Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 142
- **Related terms:** [DOWN](#down) down, [MASS](#mass) mass, [SHK](#shk) shock, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk

### DOWN

- **Full term:** Down
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** downward shock, decrease
- **Definition:** Narrows a shock or capital amount to the case where the assumption falls.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a downgrade.
- **Source checked:** Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 142
- **Related terms:** [UP](#up) up, [MASS](#mass) mass, [SHK](#shk) shock, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk

### MASS

- **Full term:** Mass
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** mass lapse
- **Definition:** Narrows a shock or capital amount to the case of a large one-off event at the start. In the Solvency II and South African standard formulas the mass lapse shock is an immediate loss of 40% of policies, or 70% for certain group business.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a gradual rise, which is UP.
- **Source checked:** Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 142(6). South African Prudential Standard FSI 4.2 section 8.5
- **Related terms:** [UP](#up) up, [DOWN](#down) down, [SHK](#shk) shock, [CAT](#cat) catastrophe risk, [LPS](#lps) lapse, [LPSR](#lpsr) lapse risk

### LPSR

- **Full term:** Lapse risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** policyholder behaviour risk, persistency risk
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk of loss from changes in the rates at which policyholders use their options to stop, surrender, reduce, restrict, renew or resume cover.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LPS, which is one narrow event. Regulatory lapse risk covers lapse, surrender, paid-up and every other policyholder option.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(3)(f), Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 142. South African Prudential Standard FSI 4.2 section 8
- **Related terms:** [LPS](#lps) lapse, [SUR](#sur) surrender, [PUP](#pup) paid-up, [DCON](#dcon) discontinuance, [WDL](#wdl) partial withdrawal, [MASS](#mass) mass, [UP](#up) up, [DOWN](#down) down, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [PERS](#pers) persistency, [SHK](#shk) shock

### REVN

- **Full term:** Revision risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount or shock to the risk that annuity benefits in payment are increased by a change in law or in the health of the person insured.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a revision of assumptions, which is ESTCH. Not IREV.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(3)(e), Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 141
- **Related terms:** [LONG](#long) longevity risk, [ANTY](#anty) annuity, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [MORB](#morb) morbidity risk, [SHK](#shk) shock

### PROP

- **Full term:** Property
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** real estate
- **Definition:** Narrows assets, returns or capital to land and buildings.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a proportion, which uses PER. Not proportional reinsurance, which is QS.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 105(5)(c)
- **Related terms:** [EQ](#eq) equity, [MKT](#mkt) market risk, [ASSET](#asset) assets, [CONC](#conc) concentration risk

### CONC

- **Full term:** Concentration risk
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount to the risk of loss from holding too much exposure to one counterparty or one group of related assets.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CPTY, which is about default. Concentration is about lack of spread.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Articles 13(35) and 105(5)(f)
- **Related terms:** [MKT](#mkt) market risk, [CPTY](#cpty) counterparty default risk, [PROP](#prop) property, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement

### LAC

- **Full term:** Loss-absorbing capacity
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** LAC TP, LAC DT
- **Definition:** Narrows a capital amount to the reduction allowed because losses would be shared with policyholders through lower future bonuses, or with the tax authority through deferred tax.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not LC (loss component). Not DIV, which arises from risks not happening together.
- **Source checked:** Solvency II Directive 2009/138/EC Article 108
- **Related terms:** [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [DIV](#div) diversification, [BON](#bon) bonus, [TAX](#tax) tax

## Profit, embedded value and new business

Measures of profit and of value to shareholders.

### PROF

- **Full term:** Profit
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** earnings, surplus arising
- **Definition:** Income less outgo less the increase in reserves in the model period, on the stated basis.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not NCF, which ignores reserves. Not CSM. Not PL, which is a statement. Not DISTE, which also allows for capital.
- **Related terms:** [NCF](#ncf) net cash flow, [DISTE](#diste) distributable earnings, [TRF](#trf) transfer, [TAX](#tax) tax, [SH](#sh) shareholder, [PL](#pl) profit or loss, [ISR](#isr) insurance service result, [EV](#ev) embedded value, [VIF](#vif) value of in-force, [VNB](#vnb) value of new business, [SURP](#surp) surplus, [NBS](#nbs) new business strain, [IRR](#irr) internal rate of return, [DPP](#dpp) discounted payback period

### DISTE

- **Full term:** Distributable earnings
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** distributable profit
- **Definition:** Profit after tax less the increase in required capital, plus investment return on that capital.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PROF. Not TRF, which is what is actually paid across. Not DIS or DISC. In embedded value reporting the shareholder cash flow of a period is the movement in free surplus.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 107 version 8 section 2.3
- **Related terms:** [PROF](#prof) profit, [TRF](#trf) transfer, [RCAP](#rcap) required capital, [FSUR](#fsur) free surplus, [VIF](#vif) value of in-force

### TRF

- **Full term:** Transfer
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** shareholder transfer, distribution
- **Definition:** An amount moved from the policyholder fund to shareholders, or between funds, in the model period.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PROF or DISTE, which are amounts available and not amounts moved.
- **Related terms:** [PROF](#prof) profit, [DISTE](#diste) distributable earnings, [SH](#sh) shareholder, [NCF](#ncf) net cash flow

### ANW

- **Full term:** Adjusted net worth
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The market value of shareholders' assets allocated to the covered business in excess of its liabilities, being required capital plus free surplus.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not OF, which is a regulatory measure.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 107 version 8 section 2.6
- **Related terms:** [EV](#ev) embedded value, [VIF](#vif) value of in-force, [FSUR](#fsur) free surplus, [RCAP](#rcap) required capital

### VIF

- **Full term:** Value of in-force
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** PVIF
- **Definition:** The present value of future after tax shareholder profits from business in force at the valuation date, before the cost of required capital is deducted.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not EV, which adds adjusted net worth and deducts the cost of required capital. Not VNB. Not CSM. Some frameworks quote it after the cost of capital, so a description must say which.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 107 version 8 sections 2.7 and 5.5
- **Related terms:** [EV](#ev) embedded value, [ANW](#anw) adjusted net worth, [VNB](#vnb) value of new business, [COC](#coc) cost of capital, [TVOG](#tvog) time value of options and guarantees, [PROF](#prof) profit, [DISTE](#diste) distributable earnings

### VNB

- **Full term:** Value of new business
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** new business value, VONB
- **Definition:** The present value at the point of sale of future after tax shareholder profits from business written in the period, less the cost of required capital.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not NB. Not VIF. Not the CSM on new business.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 107 version 8 sections 2.9 and 5.6
- **Related terms:** [NB](#nb) new business, [EV](#ev) embedded value, [VIF](#vif) value of in-force, [APE](#ape) annual premium equivalent, [PROF](#prof) profit, [NBS](#nbs) new business strain, [IRR](#irr) internal rate of return

### APE

- **Full term:** Annual premium equivalent
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** New regular premiums expressed per year plus one tenth of new single premiums.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a premium that is actually received. Not the present value of new business premiums, which is what APN 107 asks to be disclosed.
- **Source checked:** Market convention. Not defined in the sources checked
- **Related terms:** [VNB](#vnb) value of new business, [NB](#nb) new business, [PREM](#prem) premium, [SGL](#sgl) single, [REG](#reg) regular

### COC

- **Full term:** Cost of capital
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** frictional cost, cost of required capital
- **Definition:** The cost to shareholders of holding required capital. As an amount in embedded value reporting it is the required capital less the present value of its future releases and of the after tax investment return on it. With the measure RATE it is the annual rate charged on capital, as in the risk margin.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not the capital itself.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 107 version 8 section 2.8. Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 Article 39
- **Related terms:** [RCAP](#rcap) required capital, [CAP](#cap) capital, [EV](#ev) embedded value, [VIF](#vif) value of in-force, [RM](#rm) risk margin

### TVOG

- **Full term:** Time value of options and guarantees
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The extra cost of options and guarantees above their value on a single central scenario, found from stochastic or closed form methods.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not GTEE, which is the guaranteed level. Not the whole cost of guarantees, which also includes the intrinsic value already in the central projection. South African guidance values the whole guarantee reserve and does not use this term.
- **Source checked:** Market convention. For the South African treatment see ASSA APN 110 version 5
- **Related terms:** [GTEE](#gtee) guarantee, [RN](#rn) risk neutral, [EV](#ev) embedded value, [VIF](#vif) value of in-force, [BEL](#bel) best estimate liability

### FSUR

- **Full term:** Free surplus
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** free capital
- **Definition:** The market value of shareholders' assets allocated to the covered business that back neither liabilities nor required capital.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not OF. Not FV.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 107 version 8 section 2.4
- **Related terms:** [ANW](#anw) adjusted net worth, [RCAP](#rcap) required capital, [CAP](#cap) capital, [OF](#of) own funds, [DISTE](#diste) distributable earnings, [EV](#ev) embedded value, [SURP](#surp) surplus

### SURP

- **Full term:** Surplus
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** solvency margin, free assets, estate
- **Definition:** The value of assets less the value of liabilities at the stated time point, on the stated basis.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not PROF, which is the surplus arising in a period. Not FSUR, which also deducts required capital. The description must give the basis, because the answer depends on how assets and liabilities are valued.
- **Related terms:** [PROF](#prof) profit, [FSUR](#fsur) free surplus, [OF](#of) own funds, [ASSET](#asset) assets, [RES](#res) reserve, [NBS](#nbs) new business strain

### NBS

- **Full term:** New business strain
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** valuation strain
- **Definition:** The amount by which the first premium falls short of the initial expenses, commission, reserve and capital that the insurer must set up when a policy starts.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a loss. The strain is a need for finance and is expected to be repaid by later profits.
- **Related terms:** [SURP](#surp) surplus, [NB](#nb) new business, [PROF](#prof) profit, [RCAP](#rcap) required capital, [VNB](#vnb) value of new business, [DPP](#dpp) discounted payback period

### IRR

- **Full term:** Internal rate of return
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Definition:** The discount rate at which the present value of the profits from a contract or block of business is zero.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not the risk discount rate, which is an input. The internal rate of return is a result.
- **Related terms:** [DPP](#dpp) discounted payback period, [PROF](#prof) profit, [VNB](#vnb) value of new business, [DISC](#disc) discount

### DPP

- **Full term:** Discounted payback period
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** break-even period
- **Definition:** The policy duration at which the profits that have emerged so far first have a present value of zero or more.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not TERM or DUR. It is a result of a profit test and not a feature of the policy.
- **Related terms:** [IRR](#irr) internal rate of return, [PROF](#prof) profit, [DUR](#dur) policy duration, [NBS](#nbs) new business strain

## ZA extension: South Africa

Marked extension set ZA. These tokens are part of a name only when a model declares the ZA set.

### SAM

- **Full term:** Solvency Assessment and Management
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Also called:** South African solvency basis, FSI basis
- **Definition:** The informal name for the South African solvency basis. The basis is set in law by the Insurance Act 18 of 2017 and the Financial Soundness Standards for Insurers, in force from 1 July 2018.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SII, which it resembles but does not copy. Not SA or SAR. The standards themselves do not use the name SAM, which was the name of the project that produced them.
- **Source checked:** South African Prudential Standard FSI 1 to FSI 4, Prudential Authority, 2018
- **Related terms:** [SII](#sii) Solvency II, [FSV](#fsv) financial soundness valuation, [TP](#tp) technical provisions, [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [MCR](#mcr) minimum capital requirement, [RM](#rm) risk margin

### FSV

- **Full term:** Financial soundness valuation
- **Slot:** BASIS
- **Also called:** published reporting basis
- **Definition:** The South African prudently realistic valuation of liabilities: best estimate assumptions plus compulsory margins and any discretionary margins. It was set out in SAP 104, formerly PGN 104, which the Actuarial Society withdrew at the end of 2023.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FV or SV. Not SAM.
- **Source checked:** ASSA SAP 104 version 9 sections 2.5 and 3.1
- **Related terms:** [SAM](#sam) Solvency Assessment and Management, [MGN](#mgn) margin, [BSR](#bsr) bonus stabilisation reserve, [COMP](#comp) compulsory, [DISCR](#discr) discretionary, [CAR](#car) capital adequacy requirement

### FUN

- **Full term:** Funeral cover
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** funeral benefit, burial cover
- **Definition:** Narrows a benefit or claim to a small lump sum paid quickly on death to meet funeral costs, often covering several family members under one policy. In South Africa it is a legal class of business with a cap on the benefit per life.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not FV or a fund. On funeral business the number of lives and the number of policies differ, so counts must say which.
- **Source checked:** Insurance Act 18 of 2017, Schedule 2, Table 1
- **Related terms:** [HIV](#hiv) HIV and AIDS, [RETR](#retr) retrenchment, [DTH](#dth) death, [CLM](#clm) claim, [LS](#ls) lump sum, [LIFE](#life) life assured, [WAITP](#waitp) waiting period, [CRDL](#crdl) credit life

### HIV

- **Full term:** HIV and AIDS
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** AIDS extra mortality
- **Definition:** Narrows a rate or loading to the extra mortality or morbidity expected from HIV and AIDS, above the base table.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not the whole mortality rate. It is the extra part only.
- **Source checked:** ASSA APN 105 version 4
- **Related terms:** [FUN](#fun) funeral cover, [RETR](#retr) retrenchment, [DTH](#dth) death, [LOAD](#load) loading, [MORB](#morb) morbidity risk

### RETR

- **Full term:** Retrenchment
- **Slot:** ITEM or KIND
- **Also called:** involuntary unemployment, redundancy cover
- **Definition:** The life assured loses employment through no fault of their own, which triggers a benefit, usually on credit life policies. It is also a risk in the South African capital formula.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not RET (reinsurance retention). Not retirement.
- **Source checked:** South African Prudential Standard FSI 4.2 section 11. Insurance Act 18 of 2017, Schedule 2
- **Related terms:** [FUN](#fun) funeral cover, [HIV](#hiv) HIV and AIDS, [CRDL](#crdl) credit life, [CLM](#clm) claim, [RET](#ret) retention

### IPF

- **Full term:** Individual policyholder fund
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows an item to the South African tax fund for policies owned by persons other than companies, apart from policies held in the untaxed and risk policy funds.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not IF.
- **Source checked:** Income Tax Act 58 of 1962, section 29A(4)(b)
- **Related terms:** [CPF](#cpf) company policyholder fund, [UPF](#upf) untaxed policyholder fund, [RPF](#rpf) risk policy fund, [CORF](#corf) corporate fund, [TAX](#tax) tax, [PH](#ph) policyholder

### CPF

- **Full term:** Company policyholder fund
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows an item to the South African tax fund for policies owned by companies, apart from policies held in the untaxed and risk policy funds.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CPTY.
- **Source checked:** Income Tax Act 58 of 1962, section 29A(4)(c)
- **Related terms:** [IPF](#ipf) individual policyholder fund, [UPF](#upf) untaxed policyholder fund, [RPF](#rpf) risk policy fund, [CORF](#corf) corporate fund, [TAX](#tax) tax

### UPF

- **Full term:** Untaxed policyholder fund
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows an item to the South African tax fund for non-risk business of retirement funds and other exempt owners, annuities in payment and tax free investments.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not UPR.
- **Source checked:** Income Tax Act 58 of 1962, section 29A(4)(a)
- **Related terms:** [IPF](#ipf) individual policyholder fund, [CPF](#cpf) company policyholder fund, [RPF](#rpf) risk policy fund, [CORF](#corf) corporate fund, [TAX](#tax) tax, [ANTY](#anty) annuity

### RPF

- **Full term:** Risk policy fund
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Definition:** Narrows an item to the South African tax fund for risk policies issued in years of assessment that start on or after 1 January 2016, and older risk policies moved there by election.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a retirement fund.
- **Source checked:** Income Tax Act 58 of 1962, section 29A(4)(e). SARS Interpretation Note 102
- **Related terms:** [IPF](#ipf) individual policyholder fund, [CPF](#cpf) company policyholder fund, [UPF](#upf) untaxed policyholder fund, [CORF](#corf) corporate fund, [TAX](#tax) tax, [RISK](#risk) risk premium

### BSR

- **Full term:** Bonus stabilisation reserve
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** bonus smoothing reserve
- **Definition:** Surplus on smoothed bonus business that is earmarked for policyholders and not yet declared as bonus. It is the assets backing the business less the policy values declared to date.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not a reserve in the ordinary sense. A positive balance is part of the liability. A negative balance can be recognised only in part.
- **Source checked:** ASSA SAP 104 version 9 section 3.4.6
- **Related terms:** [FSV](#fsv) financial soundness valuation, [BON](#bon) bonus

### CORF

- **Full term:** Corporate fund
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** shareholders' fund for tax
- **Definition:** Narrows an item to the South African tax fund that holds all assets and liabilities of the insurer not held in the four policyholder funds.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not CPF, which holds policies owned by companies. Not SH, though the fund is where shareholder profits are taxed.
- **Source checked:** Income Tax Act 58 of 1962, section 29A(4)(d)
- **Related terms:** [IPF](#ipf) individual policyholder fund, [CPF](#cpf) company policyholder fund, [UPF](#upf) untaxed policyholder fund, [RPF](#rpf) risk policy fund, [TAX](#tax) tax, [SH](#sh) shareholder

### CRDL

- **Full term:** Credit life
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** credit protection, loan protection
- **Definition:** Narrows an item to cover that pays a debt, or its instalments, on death, disability, retrenchment or another stated event.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not credit risk, which is CPTY or SPRD.
- **Source checked:** Insurance Act 18 of 2017, Schedule 2, Table 1
- **Related terms:** [RETR](#retr) retrenchment, [FUN](#fun) funeral cover, [DTH](#dth) death, [DIS](#dis) disability

### COMP

- **Full term:** Compulsory
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** prescribed margin, first tier margin
- **Definition:** Narrows a margin to the one prescribed by South African guidance for each assumption under the financial soundness valuation.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not COMM (commission). Not DISCR, which the actuary chooses.
- **Source checked:** ASSA SAP 104 version 9 section 3.3 (withdrawn 2023)
- **Related terms:** [DISCR](#discr) discretionary, [MGN](#mgn) margin, [FSV](#fsv) financial soundness valuation

### DISCR

- **Full term:** Discretionary
- **Slot:** KIND
- **Also called:** second tier margin
- **Definition:** Narrows a margin to one the actuary chooses to hold above the compulsory margins under the financial soundness valuation.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not DISC (discount). Not COMP. Not a discretionary bonus, which is BON.
- **Source checked:** ASSA SAP 104 version 9 section 3.3 (withdrawn 2023)
- **Related terms:** [COMP](#comp) compulsory, [MGN](#mgn) margin, [FSV](#fsv) financial soundness valuation

### CAR

- **Full term:** Capital adequacy requirement
- **Slot:** ITEM
- **Also called:** CAR cover
- **Definition:** The South African capital requirement for long-term insurers before the present solvency standards. It is historic.
- **Not to be confused with:** Not SCR, which replaced it. Not CAT or CAP.
- **Source checked:** ASSA SAP 104 version 9 (withdrawn 2023)
- **Related terms:** [SCR](#scr) Solvency capital requirement, [MCR](#mcr) minimum capital requirement, [CAP](#cap) capital, [FSV](#fsv) financial soundness valuation
